Black Friday air fryer deals: what should you check before you buy?

A discount on the wrong shape is still the wrong purchase. Five minutes with a tape measure and a listing beats an evening with a returns label.

Updated 2026-11-20 · Crisp & Counter

Illustration. Not a photograph of any product sold here.

A deal only matters on the machine you would have bought anyway

The shopping weekend changes the price tag, not the decision. The format question — drawer or door, one basket or two — is exactly the same in November as it is in March, and getting it wrong is expensive at any discount.

So decide the shape first, with the guides on this site or a piece of paper, and only then go looking at what is on offer. A shortlist of one or two models is all you need; everything else is noise with a countdown on it.

The five checks that take five minutes

The format
Basket, dual-basket or oven-style. If you have not answered this yet, stop reading deals and read the shape guide first.
The measurements
Width and depth of the counter space, height to the cabinet above, and the distance to the nearest socket. Measure the space, not the feeling.
The batch reality
Write down the meal you cook most and how many people eat it. That decides capacity more than any number on the box.
The wash-up
Count the pieces that need washing: one or two drawers, or a rack, a tray and the accessories. Every piece is a piece you own forever.
The listing read
Open the product page and read the title clause by clause. The first clause is usually the job; the rest is the ambition.

What to do on the product page itself

Amazon serves the current figure, the availability and the delivery date on the listing, and those are the values to read — this site deliberately publishes none of them, because a copied number is a stale number (SRC-02). Click through from here and read them where they are live.

While you are there, check the model variant selected on the page. The same title can cover more than one size or colour, and the variant you saw in a promotion is not always the variant the page opens on. Check the return window shown on the listing too, and keep the packaging until you have measured the machine in your kitchen.

Where this site helps, and where it stops

Everything here is built to make the decision clearer before the shopping starts: the shape guide, the capacity checklist, the space audit and the cleanup guide. What this site does not do is rank machines, score them, or claim anyone cooked with them.

If the box arrives and it is wrong

It happens: the variant was wrong, the counter was optimistic, the drawer is louder than expected. Return it. An unused machine sent back promptly is a much cheaper mistake than a machine that lives in a cupboard for a year to avoid admitting the mistake.

Then write down what was wrong before you shop again. Most returns come from one of the five checks above, and the note takes thirty seconds.
