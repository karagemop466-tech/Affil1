Suggestions queue — choose after evidence

  1. Highest priority — validate links: in the enrolled Associates workflow, verify the intended ASIN/listing, record source and date, observe redirects separately, and test tracking reports before scaling.
  2. Reader trust — complete factual comparisons: after identity is confirmed, compare only sourced attributes relevant to format, capacity, cleanup and dimensions. Leave unknown cells blank.
  3. Search discovery — publish question-led guides: use the proposed titles as hypotheses; add factual sources, author/reviewer attribution, publication and update dates, then update sitemap/feed when published.
  4. Measurement — deploy after authorization: instrument privacy-preserving outbound click events and self-hosted analytics only after data access, privacy notice/consent and retention decisions. Defined events: page_view, outbound_click, guide_open, search_query_referral, report_import.
  5. Operations — gate content: checklist disclosure visibility, sponsored link rel attribute, source provenance, Amazon API/policy review, and editorial endorsement context before publishing.

Recommendation: begin with link validation and disclosure/policy review, not automated product scoring. No revenue or conversion forecasts are made.
