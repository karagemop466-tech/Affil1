Air fryer or toaster oven: which one earns the counter space?

One browns from all sides in a drawer; one toasts and bakes behind a door. The honest comparison is about space, washing, and the food you actually cook.

Updated 2026-11-20 · Crisp & Counter

Illustration of an oven-style cavity, not a photograph of a product on this site.

Same job, two different machines

Both exist to put browned, hot food on a plate without heating a full-sized oven. A basket air fryer does it in one deep container with a fan close to the food. A toaster oven does it in a shallow box with racks and a door.

The category names blur on purpose — the listing behind P-02 calls itself a French door multi-function air fryer toaster oven, in the seller's wording. What separates machines is not the badge. It is the shape of the space and what that shape lets you put inside.

What actually changes on a weeknight

Intervention
A drawer means pulling it out and shaking. A rack means turning pieces and sometimes swapping shelf positions. Pick the one that matches how patient you are at 7pm.
What fits
A shallow cavity takes a tray, a pan, a small mould, a plate of leftovers. A deep drawer mostly takes loose food. If you reheat plated food, this row decides it.
Washing
Baskets are one or two pieces. An oven arrives with a rack, a tray, sometimes an accessory set — P-02's listing states six accessories — and each one is a separate thing to wash and store.
Front clearance
A door that opens outward eats the space in front of the machine: usually the space you stand in. French doors swing sideways, which is gentler in a galley kitchen.
Height
A drawer needs room above to lift out full. Check the cabinet above before you commit.

The counter-space trade, honestly

A toaster oven is wider and shallower; a basket fryer is squarer and taller. Neither is compact in the way a kettle is compact. The machine that wins your counter is the one whose shape matches the slot you have — and the one that replaces something else beats the one that joins something else.

If a toaster oven already lives on your counter, the question becomes whether P-02-style machines would replace it outright. If nothing is being replaced, a basket machine adds capability with a smaller footprint argument.

A short way to decide

Write down the meal you cook most often and whether it needs to lie flat. If it fits in a container — fries, wings, vegetables tossed in oil — a drawer is simpler. If it needs a tray, a pan, or a door you can watch through, the oven shape is doing something a basket cannot.

Then measure the slot: width, depth including door travel, height to the cabinet, and the socket. The spec sheet after that will not change the answer.
