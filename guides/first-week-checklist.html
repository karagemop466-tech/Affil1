Your first week with a new air fryer: a checklist that is not recipes

Everything you should do in the first seven days so the appliance is still on the counter in month six — seasoning the run, learning the hot spots, and setting up the cleanup you will actually keep.

Updated 2026-11-20 · Crisp & Counter

Before you start

Most countertop fryers are abandoned in week eight, not because they cook badly but because nobody set them up. This is the setup week: five small tasks, none of them recipes.

Day 1 — burn it off, then learn its language

Run the empty appliance hot for the time the manufacturer's manual specifies, in a room you can ventilate, with nothing in the basket. New coatings and insulation off-gas; doing it once, deliberately, on day one is better than discovering it on the night you have people over. Then read the manual's own cleaning section before the first meal, because it is the only document that describes your specific basket.

Day 2 — find the hot spots with one cheap test

Cook a single layer of something small and uniform — the cheapest frozen thing in your freezer is ideal — and stop it two minutes early. Look at where browning happened first: front, back, left, right, edges. Every fan has a personality. Knowing yours is worth more than any preset, and it is the one measurement you can take in your own kitchen in ten minutes.

Day 4 — give it a fixed home

Choose the spot with three constraints: an outlet you are willing to keep reserved, rear clearance for the exhaust, and a door swing that does not hit a cabinet handle. If the machine has to be lifted out of a cupboard each time, plan for the fact that it will stop happening in about six weeks.
