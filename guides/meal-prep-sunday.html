Weeknight cooking

One basket or two for Sunday meal prep?

Sketch one batch of the lunch you actually pack before buying a bigger air fryer.

By Crisp & Counter Editorial · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read

Pick a batch you already cook

Write down one main and one side you usually pack. Lay them out in your usual pan or on a board and measure the area they take in one layer. Use that as a question to take to the appliance manual, not a capacity claim.

Compare one big space with two small ones

The Chefman 6-quart dual-basket listing describes two separate 3-quart baskets. Its TurboFry listing describes a single 8-quart basket. Don’t treat both labels as usable floor area. If you cook the same food in every batch, compare the usable space of the single basket first. If two foods need independent settings, check the controls on the dual-basket model.

Read the cleanup instructions for the exact model

The two Amazon titles describe dishwasher-safe baskets. Check the manual for the removable inserts and the parts you’ll wash between batches. A line about the basket doesn’t automatically cover every part.
