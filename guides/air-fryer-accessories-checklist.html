Which air-fryer accessories will you actually use?

The drawer fills up fast — liners, racks, tongs, mats, moulds. A short filter for what earns space and what becomes expensive clutter.

Updated 2026-11-20 · Crisp & Counter

Illustration. Not a photograph of any product sold here.

The accessory rule

An accessory earns its place only if it solves a problem you have already had twice. Everything else is clutter with a product page. The test is not “would this be useful one day” — it is “did I miss this last week”.

The second filter: check what is already in the box. P-02's listing states six cooking accessories included; P-01 and P-03 state their baskets and trays. Read the listing before buying a duplicate of something you already own.

What tends to earn its place

Liners for the cleanup
If washing up is the reason the machine sits idle, liners attack the actual problem. The cleanup guide works through what each shape needs.
Tongs that fit the drawer
Short, soft-ended tongs you can work inside a hot basket. The ones from the big-tool-jar are usually too long and too clumsy.
A mat for the counter
Heat protection for the surface under and around the machine — the boring purchase nobody regrets.
A rack only if the manual allows it
Stacking changes how air moves. If the exact model's manual says a rack or stacking is supported in the mode you use, fine. If it is silent, assume it is not a good idea.

What tends to gather dust

Unitaskers
Anything that makes one specific shape of one specific food. Fun in the product photo, unused by the third week.
Oversized inserts
If an insert shrinks the food area you actually use, it has made the machine smaller. Measure the basket before the accessory goes in the cart.
Second copies of included parts
The most common accessory purchase is a spare of something the box already contained.

Where the money usually goes further

A thermometer. Accessories change the convenience of the cook; a probe changes the result — and it is the one tool that works across every machine you will ever own. If the gift budget or the treat-yourself budget is small, that is where it goes first.

Included-accessory claims are the sellers' wording from listing titles reached on 2026-11-20. No accessory on this site was tested, and compatibility with any specific machine is a question for that machine's manual, not for this page.
