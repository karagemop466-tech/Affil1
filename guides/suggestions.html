SUGGESTIONS QUEUE · UPDATED 20 NOVEMBER 2026

Suggestions for
review.

Suggestions are collected here so someone can review them and choose the best path before any change is implemented. Nothing is automated into the site without review, and nothing is implemented without an official source where a source is required.

How to use this queue

  1. Read each suggestion with its priority, intent and open questions.
  2. Check the evidence against the official sources linked on this site and the Flagged for review page.
  3. Choose a path — implement, adapt, defer or decline — and record the decision and date below the suggestion.
  4. Only then implement, updating the sitemap, feed and disclosure placement as needed.

Gate: any change involving product data, links, disclosure, measurement or Amazon API use requires compliance review against the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and FTC endorsement guidance before publishing.

Suggestions

HIGH · VALIDATE LINKS

Suggestion: In the enrolled Associates workflow, verify each intended ASIN/listing, record the confirmation date and source, observe the redirect target in a separate field, and test tracking reports before scaling to all five products.

Why: Amazon’s ASIN definition provides a checkable identifier; intended and observed destinations must not be merged.

Open questions: Is the account enrolled and associated with this site? Which country/site does the tracking ID apply to?

Decision: Pending review — choose implement / adapt / defer / decline and record the date.

MEDIUM · FACTUAL COMPARISONS

Suggestion: After product identity is confirmed, publish comparison tables that use only sourced attributes (format, capacity, dimensions, cleanup, counter space) and leave unknown cells blank.

Why: A blank is more useful than a guess; it protects readers and the site from unverified claims.

Open questions: Which attributes matter most to the target household?

Decision: Pending review.

MEDIUM · QUESTION-LED GUIDES

Suggestion: Publish one question-led guide every two weeks (suggested working titles: “Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?”, “A no-hype checklist for comparing air-fryer capacity”, “How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs”, “Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance”). Add factual sources, author/reviewer attribution, and publication/update dates.

Why: Question-shaped titles match how real buyers search and support organic discovery without popularity claims.

Open questions: What do Search Console queries and reader interviews show is actually needed?

Decision: Pending review.

LOW · MEASUREMENT INFRASTRUCTURE

Suggestion: Turn on the privacy-preserving outbound-click counter and self-hosted analytics endpoint (five events: page_view, outbound_click, guide_open, search_query_referral, report_import) only after data access, privacy notice/consent and retention decisions are confirmed.

Why: Measurement must be authorized before it is live; the campaign ledger stays empty until authorized first-party reports exist.

Open questions: Who owns Analytics/Search Console? What is the privacy and consent basis?

Decision: Pending review.

LOW · COMPLIANCE GATE IN PUBLISHING WORKFLOW

Suggestion: Add a pre-publish checklist: disclosure above the fold and in the footer, sponsored rel attribute on outbound product links, source provenance for every table cell, Amazon API/policy review, and endorsement-context check against FTC guidance.

Why: Keeps the Associates statement visible and reviewable on every page before anything goes live.

Open questions: Who signs off on compliance for each publish?

Decision: Pending review.

Disclosure and links

The Associates Operating Agreement asks Associates to clearly and prominently state: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” This statement appears above the masthead and in the footer of the home page and this page.
