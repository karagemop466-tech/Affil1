Instant-read or wireless: which thermometer do you need?

Pick for the way you cook: a quick spot check at the end or monitoring during a longer cook.

By Crisp & Counter Editorial · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read

Decide when you need a reading

For occasional spot checks, compare the ThermoMaven digital listing as an instant-read example. If you want to monitor during a long cook, compare the CHEF iQ Sense listing as a wireless example. These are two different tasks, so start with the one you do most.

Look up limits before you leave a probe in

Check the exact manufacturer’s instructions for temperature limits, approved appliances, insertion depth, and how the probe should be secured. For a wireless model also check the app, phone, and connection requirements. Don’t assume a handheld thermometer can stay in a closed, running appliance.

Use the food safety target, not the preset

FoodSafety.gov gives different temperature targets for different foods, and some cuts need a rest time. Look up what you’re cooking in the official chart. A longer feature list isn’t a substitute for using the thermometer as directed.
