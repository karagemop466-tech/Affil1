Do you need a meat thermometer for an air fryer?

An air fryer is very good at browning and completely blind to the middle. A probe is how you fix the second part.

Updated 2026-11-20 · Crisp & Counter

Illustration. Not a photograph of any product sold here.

The problem a probe solves

Air frying is fast and dry, and the window between cooked and overdone is short — especially for chicken pieces, pork and fish. Recipes give times, but times assume a size, a starting temperature and a machine. A probe replaces all three assumptions with one reading.

It also lets you stop opening the drawer. Every time you open it, heat leaves and the timer becomes a suggestion.

Two kinds of thermometer, two jobs

Instant read
You open the appliance, insert the probe, read the number, wipe it and put it away. P-04 states a one-second reading, ±0.5℉ accuracy, and an IP67 rating, in the seller's wording. Best for quick checks and thin pieces.
Leave-in wireless
The probe stays in the food while it cooks and reports to a base or a phone. P-05 states three probes, wireless operation, a 1000°F heat rating and names air fryers among its uses, per the seller. Best for roasts, whole birds and long cooks.

Which one for an air fryer?

If you mostly cook pieces — thighs, wings, fish, vegetables — an instant read is enough and lives in a drawer. If you cook whole birds or large roasts, a leave-in probe earns its keep because you cannot read the centre without opening the door.

Check the heat rating against how you cook. A probe rated well above your cooking temperature is one less thing to think about; one rated close to it is a thing to remember.

Where the numbers come from

Safe internal temperatures are published by food safety authorities, not by appliance sellers. USDA FSIS states that visual cues like color are unreliable and that using a food thermometer placed in the thickest part away from bone, fat or gristle is the only reliable way to verify safe internal temperature (SRC-09).

The cheap upgrade

If you are choosing between a bigger air fryer and a thermometer, the thermometer is usually the better first purchase. Capacity changes how much you can cook; temperature changes whether it is any good.
