How to read that, without over-reading it

Five of five official pages agree with everything this shop publishes about them: same product, same title, same category. Of the five submitted short links, one reached its recorded listing, two fell through to Amazon's home page, and two could not be reached at all. Three sessions now hold three different accounts of the same five slugs, which is itself the finding: the short form is not a dependable way to name a product, so every buy button here is built from the observed ASIN as a plain official listing URL, and the site claims no attribution until the account reports a click.

A dated record, made inside the enrolled Associates account, of the exact ASIN the owner means to send readers to, together with the SiteStripe link for it. On that entry the tag can be switched on and the sentence above the buy button can change.

It is not proof of intent, attribution, price, rating, stock or quality. The intended ASIN stays null for all five rows: the submitted codes are nine characters and an Amazon ASIN is ten, as Amazon's own Creators API reference shows in its examples, so a slug is never written into an ASIN field.
