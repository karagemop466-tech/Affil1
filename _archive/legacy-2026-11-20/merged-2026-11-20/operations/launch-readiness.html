Design and drafting brief

Make the home page feel like the front of a kitchen publication: warm editorial photography, product formats before operations, one question per headline, and one useful next action per card. Illustrations must not pass as product photos. Use an honest editorial byline, not a fabricated tester. On mobile, keep disclosures visible before the content and put operations in a single menu and the footer.

Give the drafting agent only the approved source packet and a buyer question. Ask for the decision first, a practical example second, and a source beside each product claim. Return a separate evidence checklist and gaps; do not guess missing numbers. A draft is not a publish instruction. Check the Associates Agreement, FTC disclosure guidance and Google paid-link markup before shipping a new CTA.
