Air fryers · Dual-basket air fryer

Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer

Two independent drawers, each the size of a small basket fryer. The listing is explicit about the split: 6 quarts total means 2 x 3 quarts, not one deep 6-quart basket.

The quick take

The most useful thing about this machine, if the listing is accurate, is the split. Two 3-quart drawers means two foods at two temperatures, finishing together via a sync claim. It is not the same thing as a single large basket, and the difference decides who should buy it.

Who this shape suits

  • You regularly cook two different things at once — protein in one drawer, vegetables in the other.
  • Your household is two to four people, and 3 quarts per drawer matches how much you actually cook in one go.
  • You want the food visible without opening a door.

Check these before you buy

  • Measure your counter depth. Two drawers that pull forward need clear space in front, not just beside.
  • Ask what happens to the second drawer's timer when you open the first — that is what sync really means in practice.
  • Confirm whether both drawers can run different temperatures simultaneously, not only different times.
