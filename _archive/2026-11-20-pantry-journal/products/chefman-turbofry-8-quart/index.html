Air fryers · Single-basket air fryer

Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt Air Fryer

One deep basket, 8 quarts, four named modes. This is the least complicated shape in the set, and for a lot of kitchens that is the whole argument.

The quick take

A single basket is easy to understand and easy to clean. Eight quarts is genuinely large for one drawer, which cuts both ways: more food per batch, and a basket that takes up more of the sink when it is time to wash it.

Who this shape suits

  • You cook one thing at a time and want a big, uncomplicated basket.
  • You would rather shake a drawer than open a door and rotate two trays.
  • You have a cupboard the basket can go into when it is not in use.

Check these before you buy

  • Measure the sink. An 8-quart basket is the part that decides whether you clean it or resent it.
  • Check the height under your wall cabinets if the machine has to live under one — the lid or handle needs clearance.
  • Ask whether bake and dehydrate run at their own temperatures or reuse fry settings.
