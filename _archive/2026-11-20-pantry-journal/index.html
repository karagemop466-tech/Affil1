How this shop is run

Four commitments, and what each one costs us

Four commitments that decide what you will and will not find here.

01

No prices, on purpose

Amazon only permits a site to show price and availability if Amazon serves that data through the link, or if it arrives through the Creators API or PA API. Neither is connected here, so you will see no price, no star rating and no stock figure anywhere on this site. Click through and Amazon will show you the current numbers — which is the only place they can be correct.

Source: Associates Program Policies, section 2(b)

02

Every claim is the listing's claim

When this site says a machine holds 26 quarts or has three probes, that is the seller's own language on the listing, reproduced and labelled. We do not measure, test or repeat performance claims as our own. Statements framed as questions are exactly that: things worth checking before you buy.

Source: Associates Program Policies, section 2(b) — no misleading claims

03

One clear disclosure, before the first link

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. That sentence sits in a strip under the masthead on every page, and again in the footer, so you never reach a buy link without having passed it. The FTC asks for disclosures that are clear and conspicuous; the practical test is whether you can weigh the recommendation at the moment you read it.

Source: FTC Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking

04

Nothing about you is measured

No analytics script, no cookie, no fingerprint, no third-party tag. There is no collector running behind this site, so there is nothing for a consent banner to ask about and nothing to sell. If that changes, this page changes first.
