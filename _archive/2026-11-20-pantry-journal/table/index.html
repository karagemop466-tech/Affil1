Method, in full

What was done. Server-side page fetch with redirects followed. The final URL, document title and breadcrumb were read from the returned page. Two of the five links had been recorded by an earlier session as returning the Amazon homepage or a bot checkpoint; both returned product pages in this pass, and the conflict is recorded rather than resolved in the site's favour.

What limits it.

  • The fetch client is bot-like; Amazon served a mobile 'product summary' variant with plpRedirect=mhFallback for every resolved link. A signed-in browser may see a different page or an interstitial.
  • One observation is a point-in-time event. It does not establish a stable destination.
  • No price, rating, review count or availability was recorded. Values seen on the page belonged to carousel cards and could not be attributed to the observed listing with confidence.
  • No tracking parameter visible in any observed URL matches shopnow047c-20. Whether the redirector attaches it server-side on a real click is unverified.
