How the decision control works. Accept, Defer or Reject writes only to this browser's local storage — it records your choice for your own reference and changes nothing on the site. That is deliberate: a page that let a visitor rewrite a published policy with one click would be worse than one that does not. SG-02

Confirm link intent before publishing any outbound control

accepted & shipped

Four short links resolve to listings, but a redirect is evidence of what a link did, not proof of what the owner meant it to be.

Recommended path
Publish the four observed products using the observed ASIN's canonical /dp/ URL plus the supplied tag. Keep intended_asin null until the owner confirms. Show the record publicly.
Alternatives considered
Treat the redirect as the intended ASIN (rejected: merges evidence into intent) · Publish nothing until confirmation (rejected: leaves the site empty, and the observation is strong evidence)
Constrained by
S02, S22
Effort
S · Risk: Medium. If a redirect later changes, a published product page points at the wrong item. Mitigated by the dated observation record.
Success measure
Five intended_asin fields filled from owner statements.

SG-04

Publish guides as product questions, not as buying-guide listicles

accepted & shipped

Generic 'best air fryer 2026' listicles compete with wirecutter-scale domains on day one and win nothing.

Recommended path
Four narrow, question-shaped pieces that a comparison shopper actually types: basket vs. oven style, a capacity checklist, a cleanup and counter-space trade-off piece, and pre-purchase questions for multi-function appliances.
Alternatives considered
Publish a single long listicle (rejected: no ranking foothold) · Publish 20 thin pages (rejected: dilutes the site and risks the original-content requirement)
Constrained by
S14, S19
Effort
M · Risk: Medium. The topics are editorial hypotheses, not validated demand. Measure before scaling.
Success measure
Impressions and query data in Search Console, once connected.

SG-05

Count outbound clicks on our own origin, never by redirecting

accepted & shipped

Click measurement usually means routing the user through a tracker, which risks the redirecting-link and URL-obscuring rules.

Recommended path
A fire-and-forget beacon on click to a write-only first-party endpoint. The anchor's real href is the Amazon URL. No redirect, no cloaking, no per-user identifier.
Alternatives considered
Server-side 302 redirect (rejected: S08, S09) · Third-party analytics script (rejected: adds a data processor and a consent burden) · No measurement (rejected: the brief requires measurement)
Constrained by
S08, S09, S06
Effort
M · Risk: Low technically; the endpoint must exist before counts are non-zero.
Success measure
Non-zero recorded click events from real sessions.

SG-07

accepted & shipped

Recommended path

If you only read one

SG-02 is the one that matters most. Four links resolve to real listings and could be published immediately on that evidence — but a redirect tells you what a link did, not what someone meant. Confirming the intended ASIN for each link is the single highest-value input available, because it converts four observed only rows into four confirmed ones and lets the copy describe a chosen product instead of a discovered one.
