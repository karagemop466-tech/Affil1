Why an empty table is the honest table

There are no conversions to report. No campaign has run, no Associates report has been pulled, and the click counter is not connected. A filled-in table at this point would be a forecast wearing a measurement's clothes, so the values are absent and the schema is published instead.

The rule for adding a row

A row may be added only when all four of these are true:

1The figure comes from an authorized first-party report — an Amazon Associates report, or this site's own click counter once it is connected.
2The report name and pull date are recorded in the provenance column.
3No field is derived. If a report does not state eligible sessions, that cell stays empty.
4Nobody has to guess what a number means. If the definition is unclear, the row waits.
Two timing facts that shape this. Commission income is paid roughly 60 days after the month in which it is earned (S11), so a reported-order figure lags the click by about two months and any table that fills in same-week conversions is not reporting Amazon's numbers. And a qualifying session ends after 24 hours, at order placement, or when the reader clicks another associate's link (S10) — so 'sessions' in this ledger means something narrower than 'visits'.
