How to decide on this one

Oven-style means you can see the food

A door and a light let you watch without disturbing the cook. That is the single biggest day-to-day difference from a basket machine, and it is the reason oven-style units suit people who bake and roast more than they fry.

Capacity you can actually fill

Twenty-six quarts is enough for a sheet pan, a small roast, or several racks at once. But large capacity only pays off if your recipes use it. A single person cooking one portion will heat a big cavity to cook a small amount.

Accessories are part of the purchase

Six accessories included changes the real cost comparison against a bare oven. Check what is in the box before comparing against a unit that charges extra for the same racks.
