Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Working title — not a claim about popularity, search volume or product superiority.

“Ten-in-one” sounds efficient until you use two functions once. Ask these before paying for modes you won’t touch:

  • Which functions will I actually use weekly? Air fry, bake, roast, reheat — or just one or two of them?
  • Does temperature range and control match those functions? Baking needs steadier, lower heat than air frying.
  • What do accessories cost, and are they dishwasher-safe? Extra racks and baskets add up fast.
  • Does one appliance really replace four, or does it just do four things adequately?
  • Where will it live? Multi-function ovens are large; confirm counter space and door swing first.
