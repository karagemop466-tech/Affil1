DECISION DESK · PRE-DECISION QUESTIONS

Questions, information,
and honest blanks.

Before any decision on this site can reasonably be made, these questions need answers. Each row shows what we already have, what we don't have, the best answer available today, and the suggested way to discover the missing piece. Blanks are recorded as blanks — never filled in.

Q1 · What exactly are we selling links to?

Answered as of 20 Nov 2026

What is each product, and is the destination real?

Have: five submitted short links (link.amazon/...). Verified: four resolve live to official Amazon product pages — B0CDY277D3 (CHEFMAN 6 Qt Dual Basket), B09B7SB46R (Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door), B08DKYBTPH (Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt), B0DZFD6R9Z (CHEF iQ Sense thermometer); titles taken from the pages themselves. Missing: one link (B0c8moW59) lands on a bot checkpoint — listing unknown. Discover: manual click in a normal browser session; then the enrolled Associates SiteStripe test.

Q2 · Do the links actually earn and report?

Unanswered — blocks scaling

Does tag shopnow047c-20 attribute to these links in first-party reports?

Have: the tracking ID (operator-selected) and outbound links formatted as Special Links with the tag appended. Missing: enrollment confirmation, site association, and a tracking report showing a test click. Discover: in the enrolled Associates account — generate one link per product with SiteStripe, click it, and read the tracking report the next day. Blocks: any promotion spend or scaling.

Q3 · What may we display — prices, ratings, reviews?

Answered — policy constraint adopted

What data can this static site show without violating policy?

Have: the policy position: price display requires Amazon-served data or approved API access; review claims need traceable provenance. So the site shows identities and verification status only — no prices, no ratings, no scraped review language. Missing: approved data channel (SiteStripe/PA API). Discover: Associates account → Product Advertising API application status; adopt suggestion S-07 only if approved.

Q4 · Who is the reader, and what do they search for?

Unanswered — blocks topic selection

Which questions do real buyers type before buying?

Have: seven editorial hypotheses published as guides (format, capacity, cleanup, multi-function, accessories, running costs, holiday counter math). Missing: any measured query, impression or click data. Discover: verify the Search Console property (S-03), wait for data after submission, then pick the next topic from the monthly export (S-10) — not from instinct.

Q5 · Are clicks and conversions measurable yet?

Partly answered

Is the measurement machinery real and permitted?

Have: a five-event client collector (page_view, outbound_click, guide_read, suggestion_review, session_start) writing to localStorage, with two self-hosted endpoint implementations ready; the campaign ledger is defined and empty by design. Missing: a deployed endpoint, a privacy notice review, and authorized first-party reports. Discover: deploy the collector on any small host, sign off the privacy notice, then populate the ledger only from authorized reports (S-05 → S-06).

Q6 · Is the operation compliant as it stands?

Answered — with flagged residuals

Do disclosure, link marking and data handling meet the operating requirements?

Have: the exact Associates statement above the fold on every page and in every footer; rel="nofollow sponsored" on every outbound product link; dated provenance for every verification claim; compliance citations (Operating Agreement §5, Program Policies, FTC Endorsement Guides). Missing: account-level confirmation that the link format is acceptable (FLG-003) — deliberately neither asserted valid nor invalid. Discover: the S-01 test in the enrolled workflow; keep PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md as the gate for every future page.

Q7 · What does the site itself still lack?

Partly answered

Which polish items remain before this reads fully "published"?

Have: brand, imagery, navigation, seven guides, a shop-style shortlist, seasonal cluster, decision desk, flagged review, feeds and sitemap. Missing: author bylines (S-08), reader capture (FLG-012 — needs a provider + privacy review), and a Search Console feedback loop (S-03). Discover: work the suggestions queue top-down; each item names its gate.

The rule this page enforces

A decision is reasonable when its blocking questions are answered from named sources, dated. Everything else waits. Proposals for acting on these answers live in the suggestions queue; the raw verification evidence lives in Flagged for review and data/confirmations.json.
