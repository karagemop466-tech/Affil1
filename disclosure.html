Affiliate disclosure

Amazon Associates statement. Affil1 is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Associates tracking ID: shopnow047c-20.

What you’ll see on this site

  • Disclosure above the fold. An affiliate disclosure strip appears under the masthead on every page, and is repeated in the footer.
  • Sponsored links. Every outbound product link is marked rel="nofollow sponsored" and visually labeled “sponsored,” so buyers and search engines know it is an affiliate link.
  • No endorsements. Product findings are not endorsements. Listings are verified against Amazon.com, but prices, ratings and availability are not guaranteed — check the listing page before buying.

Why prices and ratings are blank

Amazon permits price and availability display only where Amazon serves the link carrying the data, or where it arrives through the Creators API or PA API (Program Policies 2(b), official source). No such feed is connected here, so no price, rating, review count or availability figure appears anywhere on this site. This is a rule, not an unfinished task.

Verification standards applied

  • Each submitted Amazon short link is fetched and resolved against the official Amazon site.
  • The intended ASIN (short-link slug) and the observed redirect target are stored in separate fields and never merged, per Amazon’s ASIN definition as a checkable product identifier.
  • Values not confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged; nothing is inferred.
  • One link per product is tested in the enrolled Associates workflow (destination and tracking report) before scaling.
