About this site, and how it decides what to publish

A short, checkable account of the editorial rule, the disclosure, and why several obvious things are missing.

The disclosure

Crisp & Counter participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. When you follow a link to Amazon from this site, we may receive a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it does not change what Amazon charges you.

The disclosure appears as a strip under the masthead on every page of this site, and again in the footer. That placement is deliberate. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission asks that an affiliate relationship be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, so that a reader can decide how much weight to give the recommendation. A disclosure buried at the bottom of a long page does not do that job.

Every outbound link to Amazon on this site is marked as a paid link, carries rel="sponsored", and opens in a new tab with that fact announced to screen readers. We do not use link cloaking, and we do not use a redirect that hides where a link goes.

Why so much is blank

Three separate rules produce the white space on this site, and it is worth keeping them apart.

  • Prices, ratings and stock are prohibited without a feed. Amazon permits price and availability display only when Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or when the figure arrives through the Creators API or PA API. No such feed is connected here, so no price appears. A price copied from a page today is wrong within weeks, and wrong prices about a named product are a real problem, not a cosmetic one.
  • Specifications could not be confirmed. Wattage, dimensions, weight, warranty and noise are not stated in a citable form for these units with the access available. They are omitted rather than estimated.
  • We have not tested anything. So we do not rank the products or tell you which cooks better. A verdict without a test is a guess wearing a lab coat.

The editorial rule

One rule governs every sentence about a product on this site: a capability is stated only if it appears in the seller's own listing title. When you read “6 quart, two baskets,” that is the seller's claim, quoted. When you read “check the swing of the door before you buy,” that is advice about your kitchen, which needs no sourcing because it makes no claim about the product.

Where a listing claim could be read as a measured fact and would mislead if it were, the guide says so in the same paragraph. Where the site cannot answer a question a reader will have, the guide names the question and tells you where to find the answer.

How the links are handled

Five links were supplied for this project. Each was followed and the destination was read. Four resolved to an identifiable listing; one did not, and rather than guess at it, that gap is published on the Flagged for review page.

Two identifiers are kept in strictly separate fields for every product: the code that appeared in the supplied link, and the ASIN that the destination page actually carries. They are never merged, because merging them would destroy the only record that could catch a link pointing at the wrong product. On this set they are never the same string — the supplied codes are nine characters, and an ASIN is ten.

Privacy and cookies

This site sets no cookies, writes nothing to your browser's storage, and loads no third-party script. There is no analytics running, because there is nowhere on a static host for a first-party collector to run and a third-party one was judged not worth the disclosure surface before there is traffic to measure. The measurement plan is published on the source register so that when it is switched on, you can see exactly what it would collect.

Clicking a product link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own privacy notice applies. We receive no information about what you do after you leave this site.

Corrections

Everything factual on this site is traceable to an official page, listed on the source register. If something here is wrong, the more useful response is to say which row and which source contradicts it. Corrections are made to the data, and the page is rebuilt from the data, so a fix lands everywhere at once rather than on one page and not another.

What this site is not

It is not affiliated with Amazon beyond participation in the Associates Program. It is not a review site in the sense of having tested anything. It is not professional advice. It is a set of buying guides that would rather leave a cell empty than fill it with something you cannot check.
