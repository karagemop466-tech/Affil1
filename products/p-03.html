What it is, in plain terms

One deep basket, 8 quarts of it, and no second drawer to think about. The straightforward choice if dinner is usually one thing at a time.

Eight quarts in one basket is a genuinely different shape from six in two. One needs shaking; two need coordinating.

Who it suits

  • You cook one thing at a time and want the largest single basket here.
  • You want fewer parts to wash than a dual-basket machine.
  • You care about a high-heat setting for crispness, in the seller's wording.

Look elsewhere if

You regularly cook two different foods that need different times. You will be running it twice.
