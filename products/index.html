Before you buy from any of them

Four questions worth two minutes

  1. Measure the gap, not the machine. Width, depth and height of the space, including the room a door or drawer needs while it is open.
  2. Name the meal you cook most. Then check whether it fits in one layer with air around it. That is your real capacity, whatever the quarts say.
  3. Count what you will wash. Baskets, trays, racks and probes are all separate objects with separate jobs and separate sink space.
  4. Check the care instructions in the manual, not the listing. A claim that a basket is dishwasher-safe is a seller statement until the manufacturer's own document says it for your model.
