Reading the register

Four things worth knowing

  1. The codes are nine characters. Every supplied slug is 9 characters and contains lowercase letters; every ASIN that loaded is 10 and uppercase. The codes are short-link slugs, so the intended ASIN column stays empty rather than repeating the slug in a column labelled ASIN.
  2. The links are tagged by this site, not by the shortener. None of the five final URLs contained a tag parameter. Every outbound product link here is /dp/ASIN?tag=shopnow047c-20, which is the form Program Policies 2(b) describes. Whether the ID itself is active is a separate question (Q-07).
  3. Two products are not air fryers. P-04 and P-05 are meat thermometers, filed under a different category path. No page on this site calls them air fryers.
  4. The empty columns are the honest ones. Price, rating, review count and availability would be the most useful columns here and are absent because Amazon permits them only from data Amazon serves or via its API. The rule, quoted.
