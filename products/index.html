Reading the register

Four things a reviewer will want to check

  1. The codes are nine characters. B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59 and B0f3GKalj all contain lowercase letters and are all nine characters long. Every ASIN that loaded is ten and uppercase. The codes are short-link slugs, and the earlier version of this register was wrong to treat them as identifiers.
  2. Attribution is unconfirmed. No arrived-at page showed a tag parameter. That does not prove the links are untagged, because a redirect service may apply the tag server-side. It does mean this build cannot confirm that a click is credited to shopnow047c-20.
  3. Identity is not quality. Knowing which product a link opens says nothing about whether the product is any good. No product here was handled.
  4. The empty columns are deliberate. Price, rating, review count and stock status would all require either Amazon-served data or API access. Neither is connected, so they stay empty site-wide.
