Two of these are thermometers

P-04 resolves to a ThermoMaven instant-read probe. P-05 resolves to a CHEF iQ wireless probe set with three probes and a stated 1000°F rating. Amazon's own breadcrumb files both under Kitchen Utensils & Gadgets, then Thermometers & Timers, then Meat. The other three are under Small Appliances, then Fryers, then Air Fryers.

That is not a problem with the links. It would be a problem with this site if it presented either one as the fourth air fryer, which is exactly what a site written from the brief alone would have done. They get their own cluster and their own guide, because an instant-read probe and a wireless multi-probe set are a genuinely different decision.

Reading the register

Four things a reviewer will want to check

  1. The codes are nine characters. B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59 and B0f3GKalj all contain lowercase letters and are all nine characters long. Every ASIN that loaded is ten and uppercase. The codes are short-link slugs, and the earlier version of this register was wrong to treat them as identifiers.
  2. Attribution is now built in, and still unconfirmed. No arrival URL carried a tag parameter, so the site stopped offering the short links as click targets and instead offers /dp/<ASIN>/?tag=shopnow047c-20, which is the format Program Policies 2(b) requires. Whether Amazon records such a click is confirmed only by the click report, and that is FLG-002.
  3. Identity is not quality. Knowing which product a link opens says nothing about whether the product is any good. No product here was handled.
  4. The empty columns are deliberate. Price, rating, review count and stock status would all require either Amazon-served data or API access. Neither is connected, so they stay empty site-wide.
