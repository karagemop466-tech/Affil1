What it is, in plain terms

Three probes that stay in the food while it cooks, so you can watch the middle come up to temperature instead of opening the oven to check. The seller also lists air fryers among its uses.

This is a different category from the air fryers, and the site keeps it in its own section. A thermometer is a measuring tool you move between appliances; an air fryer is an appliance.

Who it suits

  • You roast or smoke large pieces and want to watch without opening the door.
  • You cook more than one piece at once and want a probe in each.
  • You are comfortable with a phone in the kitchen.

Look elsewhere if

You want a thermometer you pick up, read, and put away. Set-up and charging are part of owning this.
