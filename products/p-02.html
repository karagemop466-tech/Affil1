What it is, in plain terms

The one that replaces a toaster oven rather than sitting beside it. Doors that open outward, racks instead of a drawer, and room for a tray of vegetables or a small pan.

A shallow cavity changes what fits: trays, racks and pans work, loose food needs a basket accessory. Count the accessories, because each one is something to wash and store.

Who it suits

  • You want one box that air-fries, toasts, bakes and reheats.
  • You cook for three or more and need more than one layer at a time.
  • You have clear counter in front of it for the doors to open into.

Look elsewhere if

Your counter is tight, or you mostly cook one basket of chips at a time. This is the least compact way to fry.
