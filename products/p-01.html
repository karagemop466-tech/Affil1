What it is, in plain terms

Two independent drawers are the whole argument. Chips in one, chicken in the other, and the sync-finish setting is there so they land at the same time instead of one going cold.

The 6-quart figure is two 3-quart drawers, not one big one. Compare a single drawer against another single drawer, never the total against a single-basket machine.

Who it suits

  • You cook two different things at once and want them to finish together.
  • You would rather shake a drawer than turn pieces on a rack.
  • You have about the footprint of a microwave to give it.

Look elsewhere if

You want to bake, reheat a plate, or put a pan inside. A drawer is a container, not a cavity.
