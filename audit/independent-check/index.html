Independent check of the published edition

A second pair of eyes on the same five products, run on 2026-11-20 by an automated session (arena/06db21d4-affil1) that did not build the pages it is checking. It re-reads the committed HTML rather than trusting any claim made about it, and it publishes what it found — including two findings the published edition had not recorded.

For the owner, in one paragraph

The published edition is internally consistent: every internal link resolves, every page carries the disclosure before its main content, no price or rating is published anywhere, and the sitemap and feed are valid. Two commercial gaps remain, and both are deliberate rather than accidental — they are recorded below as A-TAG and A-SPON. Neither can be closed without the Associates account.

What this check did not do

It did not sign in to the Associates account, so it cannot confirm attribution, commission rates, or whether any click earns. It did not buy anything. It did not test any appliance — no appliance in this set has been handled by anyone writing for this site, and every product claim in the published edition is a quotation from a listing title. It also did not re-verify the legacy editions kept in archive/, crisp/ and legacy/: those are excluded from indexing and are historical records, not current claims.

Method

Every canonical HTML file was read from disk. Internal references were resolved against the filesystem. Amazon links were counted and inspected for rel values and for the tag= parameter. Commerce figures were searched for by pattern, with policy quotations excluded by context. Sitemap and feed were parsed as XML, then each URL was mapped back to a file. The report below is the raw result, regenerated by python3 tools/audit_canonical.py --write so it can be reproduced.
