For the owner, in one paragraph

The edition is internally consistent: every internal reference resolves, every page carries the Associates statement before its main content, no price or rating is published anywhere, and the sitemap and feed are valid. The commercial gap that remains is single and deliberate — no Amazon product link on this site carries the Associates tag, because none can until an enrolled Special Link exists. That is recorded below rather than quietly fixed, because appending a tag to a listing URL is not the same as an enrolled link.

What this check did not do

It did not sign in to the Associates account, so it cannot confirm attribution, commission rates, or whether any click earns. It did not buy anything. It did not test any appliance — no appliance in this set has been handled by anyone writing for this site, and every product claim in the edition is a quotation from a listing title. It also did not re-verify the archived editions under archive/: those are kept as historical records and are excluded from the published staging step, not silently deleted.

Method

Every canonical HTML file is read from disk. Internal references are resolved against the filesystem, with fragments and querystrings stripped. Amazon links are counted and inspected for rel values and for the tag= parameter. Commerce figures are searched for by pattern, with policy quotations excluded by their surrounding context. Sitemap and feed are parsed as XML, then each URL is mapped back to a file. The register is read directly, and the intended-versus-observed identifier split is asserted rather than assumed.

Reproduce it

python3 tools/audit_canonical.py prints the same findings to a terminal without writing anything. python3 tools/build.py regenerates this page from those findings, and the build's own reproducibility step fails if the committed copy differs from the generated one.
