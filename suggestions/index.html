Suggestion board

29 proposals, scored, with what each one would change and what it would need before it is safe to ship. 5 are blocked on things only the account owner can supply, 4 await a product decision, and 3 are recorded as things this site must never build. Read the scores as an argument, not a gospel: impact is judged against this site's purpose, not against a generic growth playbook.

How to review this board. Start with the two rows marked rejected and blocked on account — those are the ones where acting without information would cost money or trust. Then pick by the ratio of impact to effort. Nothing on this page has been implemented silently; what shipped is labelled shipped and says so.

Filter
SUG-01

Publish the disclosure in a strip under the masthead, and again in the footer

shipped
impact 5/5 effort 1/5 confidence high — it is a stated obligation, not a hypothesis

WhyBoth Amazon's Operating Agreement and the FTC's guidance put the duty on the publisher, and the FTC's test is whether a reader can weigh the recommendation at the moment they read it. A disclosure only in the footer fails that test on a long page.

Evidence needednone; the sources are already read and quoted

What shippedPresent on every page, generated by one template, so a new page cannot ship without it.

SUG-02

Stop publishing prices, star ratings and review counts entirely

shipped
impact 5/5 effort 1/5 confidence high

WhyProgram Policies 2(b) allows price display only when Amazon serves the link carrying the data, or when it comes from the Creators API / PA API. This site has no such feed. A stale price is also a misrepresentation risk under the same section.

Evidence needednone

What shippedNo number appears on any product surface. The blank is deliberate and is explained in-line, not hidden.

SUG-03

Keep intended ASIN and observed redirect target as two separate, never-merged fields

shipped
impact 5/5 effort 1/5 confidence high

WhyThe observed redirect is a fact about today's redirect. The intended ASIN is a fact about what the owner meant to enrol. Merging them destroys the only audit trail that would catch a wrong-product redirect.

Evidence needednone

What shippedproducts.json carries them in separate keys. intended_asin is deliberately null for all five rows.

SUG-04

Build the landing page as a publication front page, with the audit surfaces demoted to the footer

shipped
impact 4/5 effort 3/5 confidence medium — standard practice, not measured on this site

WhyA buyer arriving from a search result needs a reason to stay within about five seconds. A verification table is a reason to leave. The register still has to exist; it just should not be the front door.

Evidence neededpost-launch: scroll depth and outbound click rate by page

What shippedFront page leads with the products and the guides. Every register is reachable from the footer.

SUG-05

Write question-shaped guides, one per real buyer decision, and link products from inside the argument

shipped
impact 4/5 effort 3/5 confidence medium — the titles are hypotheses until query data exists

WhyA guide that answers a question earns a link and a share; a product card does not. Question-shaped titles also match how people type into a search box.

Evidence neededSearch Console query data after 4–6 weeks

What shippedFour guides published. Every claim inside them is either quoted from a listing title or omitted.

SUG-06

Ship an RSS feed and one canonical host, before chasing traffic

shipped
impact 3/5 effort 1/5 confidence high on the mechanics, medium on the size of the effect

WhySyndication gives a second surface for free and makes the publishing cadence visible. Duplicate hosts split whatever authority the site earns.

Evidence needednone for the mechanics

What shippedfeed.xml plus rel=alternate on every page, canonical tags throughout, robots.txt pointing at the sitemap.

SUG-07

Define the five events and a privacy-preserving outbound-click counter — but deploy nothing until a host is chosen

blocked on decision

WhyMeasurement has to exist before optimisation, or the next decision is made on instinct. But an unhosted endpoint and an unconsented cookie are worse than no measurement: one is dead code, the other is a compliance problem.

Evidence neededa first-party host (see FLG-009)

Why it is blockedThe schema is published in events.json. The collector is a documented design, not live code. No third-party script is loaded on this site.

WhyIf the tag is dropping, one wrong link and five wrong links cost the same in lost trust but five is a bigger hole. One-link-per-product, confirmed in the Associates report, is the cheapest possible experiment.

Evidence neededan Associates dashboard session

Why it is blockedRequires reading the click report in the Associates dashboard. This session has no account access.

SUG-09

shipped

Evidence needednone for the form-factor comparison; dimensions would need a feed

What shippedThe compare page compares form factor, what the seller claims, and what to check in person. It does not invent a winner.

SUG-11

Add author identity and a methodology page before adding more content

blocked on decision
impact 4/5 effort 2/5 confidence medium-high

WhyReaders and search engines both discount anonymous product advice. An author page with a real name, a stated method and a correction policy is the cheapest credibility upgrade available.

Evidence neededa real name and a real bio from the owner

Why it is blockedA method page exists. An author page does not, because inventing a persona would be exactly the failure mode this project is trying to avoid.

SUG-12

Publish one guide every two weeks rather than five at once

proposed
impact 3/5 effort 2/5 confidence medium

WhyA cadence is a signal that the site is maintained. A burst followed by silence reads as abandoned, and it also makes it impossible to attribute a change in traffic to a topic.

Evidence neededSearch Console data to confirm the cadence is producing indexed pages

NoteNo new guides should be drafted until SUG-08 resolves, so that the first guide is measured against a working link.

SUG-13

Add a reader-capture email list

blocked on decision
impact 3/5 effort 4/5 confidence medium

WhyAn email list is the only audience asset that survives an algorithm change. It is also the highest-risk item on this list, because it brings CAN-SPAM, consent records and a provider into scope at once.

Evidence neededa provider, a privacy review, and an unsubscribe path

Why it is blockedNot built. A signup box that silently does nothing is worse than no signup box.

SUG-14

Connect the Creators API or PA API and serve live price and availability

blocked on account
impact 5/5 effort 4/5 confidence high on the value, high on the constraint

WhyThis is the only route that makes a price legal to display, and price is the single most-requested datum on a buying page. It also unlocks real availability, which prevents sending readers to an out-of-stock listing.

Evidence neededAssociates account approval plus API credentials

Why it is blockedUntil then the correct behaviour is to show no price. Do not fill this gap with a scrape.

WhyArticle and BreadcrumbList markup help a search engine describe the page correctly. Product and Offer markup is deliberately excluded, because marking up an offer implies price and availability data this site does not have.

Evidence neededrich-result eligibility check after indexing

SUG-19

Finish the illustration system so every guide opens with a real image

proposed
impact 4/5 effort 2/5 confidence medium — it improves the perceived quality and click-through of a page; it does not by itself earn a ranking

WhyA text-only page is the single most common tell of a generated site. Five hand-commissioned-style illustrations now run on the front page, but seven of the nine guides still open with text. Consistent artwork is also the difference between a page a reader screenshots and shares and one they do not, and it is what makes a card legible in a search result or a feed reader.

Evidence neededOne art direction decision (palette and composition are already fixed in assets/css/site.css), then ~15 minutes per remaining guide.

NoteIllustrations are made for this publication and captioned as such. They must never be swapped for Amazon listing photos: see SUG-25.

SUG-20

Rotate a seasonal shelf by date, not by rebuild

proposed
impact 3/5 effort 3/5 confidence medium

WhyKitchen-gear intent is seasonal: Thanksgiving and holiday hosting now, party appetisers in December, meal-prep in January, grill-adjacent gear in May. A shelf that swaps with the calendar keeps the front page current without touching the data layer, and it puts the right question in front of the right month.

Evidence neededDecide whether a static build (re-run tools/build.py on a schedule) or a small client-side date check is acceptable. If the front page must be identical for every visitor — which is what a crawler sees — the build must re-run, not the browser.

NoteThe first seasonal feature is live now (the holiday overflow guide). What is not built is the rotation.

SUG-21

Build hub pages around situations, not superlatives

proposed
impact 4/5 effort 3/5 confidence high that it is the only compliant version of the idea; medium on traffic

Why&ldquo;Best air fryer 2026&rdquo; is the query with volume, and it is the one this site cannot answer honestly: ranking products requires either hands-on testing we have not done or third-party scores we are not licensed to republish. A hub page for &ldquo;a small kitchen that cooks for two&rdquo; or &ldquo;one that has to fit a sheet pan&rdquo; can be answered from listing facts, is the same search intent, and is defensible line by line.

Evidence neededTwo or three hubs, each assembled from fields already in src/data/products.json, plus one sentence per claim naming its source.

SUG-23

proposed

WhyQuestion-shaped guides already read like answers; marking them up lets a search engine see the question and the answer as a pair. Five new guides ship with it today. The four earlier guides have the questions in prose but no markup, so the set is inconsistent.

SUG-24

Weekly automated audit of links, disclosure and claims

shipped
impact 4/5 effort 2/5 confidence high

WhyThe compliance state of this site is only true for the hour it was built. A listing disappears, a redirect changes, a page ships without the disclosure strip — and the site is silently non-compliant. tools/validate.py already checks all of that in a single pass; running it weekly converts a one-off good state into an ongoing one.

Evidence neededA scheduled workflow running build + validate and opening an issue on failure. No secrets needed; no data leaves the repository.

What shippedBoth halves are in the repository now: .github/workflows/pages.yml rebuilds from the data layer and runs tools/validate.py before publishing anything, and weekly-audit.yml re-runs the same gate on a schedule without deploying. What is deliberately not automated: watching Amazon's own pages, which no script in this repository should be doing.

SUG-25

Do not display Amazon listing images, copy listing descriptions, or republish ratings

rejected
impact 5/5 effort 1/5 confidence high — it is a stated restriction, not a judgement call

WhyProgram Content — images, descriptions, ratings and review text — may be used only as provided through Amazon's own linking tools or the Product Advertising / Creators API, and ratings and customer reviews only when obtained through that API. Hand-copied screenshots and pasted bullet points are the most common cause of an account closing, and the risk is highest on a site that looks like it has a feed but does not.

Evidence neededNothing. This is a prohibition to observe, recorded here so the next session does not &lsquo;improve&rsquo; the product cards by scraping.

Why notRejected means: do not build it. If API access is ever granted, the replacement is a live pull with a date and time stamp beside the price, not a static copy.

SUG-26

Do not build price history, price-drop alerts or a comparison engine

rejected
impact 5/5 effort 1/5 confidence high

WhyThe Participation Requirements state that, unless otherwise agreed by Amazon, an Associate site must not have price-tracking or price-alerting functionality. A price-alert feature is the standard affiliate playbook and the standard account-closing move. The FTC's concern points the same way: an alert that goes out with stale numbers is a misleading claim sent to someone who trusted you.

Evidence neededNothing; the restriction is published. Recorded so the absence of price data reads as a policy decision and not an oversight.

Why notRejected means: this is not on the roadmap. Every blank price cell on the site is the visible consequence of obeying it.

SUG-27

Turn the ledger into an importer, with the report as the only input

blocked on account
impact 4/5 effort 3/5 confidence high

WhyThe campaign ledger is empty by design and must stay that way until numbers arrive from the enrolled account's own reports. The safe automation is a one-way importer that reads an exported report, appends rows with a timestamp and a provenance field, and refuses to compute anything not present in the file.

Evidence neededReport access, plus an agreed export format. Never scrape the dashboard into a database — that is a separate restriction and an unnecessary risk.

Why it is blockedNo authorized report has ever been read. Any session that fills this table before then is fabricating data, which is why the empty state is published rather than removed.

SUG-28

Name a human editor, with a bio that survives an audit

blocked on decision
impact 5/5 effort 2/5 confidence high

WhyE-E-A-T is not a ranking factor you can install, but a publication with no named person behind it is indistinguishable from a content farm to both a reader and a quality rater. The byline currently says &lsquo;editorial desk&rsquo; on purpose: inventing a person who has tested products would be a fabricated endorsement, which is exactly what the FTC's guides prohibit.

Evidence neededOne real person who agrees to be named, two true sentences about what they have actually done, and a corrections record they own.

Why it is blockedOnly the site owner can supply this. Until then /about/#who says the byline is a desk, and the site claims no testing.

SUG-29

Publish a downloadable capacity worksheet as the linkable asset

proposed
impact 3/5 effort 2/5 confidence medium — outreach-driven, and the effect is not measurable until Search Console is verified

WhyGuides get read; assets get linked. A one-page worksheet — measure your pan, count your one-layer portions, list the three dishes that would move off your oven — is genuinely useful, costs nothing to maintain, and is the kind of thing a niche newsletter or forum answer cites. It also sends readers back to the register with a question they can now answer.

Evidence neededA print-ready PDF generated from the same data as the capacity guide, and a decision about whether to accept links at all (no paid links, no guest-post farms).

Note

The three that need a decision from you first

  1. Connect the API, or accept no prices forever. Without the Creators API or PA API the site cannot legally show a price, and price is the number readers want most. This is the single highest-leverage unblock on the board.
  2. Run the one-link test. One product, one link, confirmed in the Associates click report. Everything downstream — scaling to five, writing more guides, buying a domain — is premature until that single click is observed.
  3. Give the site an author. A name and two sentences buy more credibility than any redesign, and it is the one thing that cannot be generated.
  4. Decide the traffic machinery. Verifying Search Console (SUG-22) and scheduling the weekly audit (SUG-24) are both one-off jobs that turn this from a published site into a measured one. Neither needs a new feature built, and until the first is done, every statement on this site about what readers want is reasoning rather than evidence.

If you only do three things. Replace the five buy links with Special Links generated inside Associates Central and confirm one click in the report; verify the Search Console property; then let four weeks of queries re-order the editorial queue before writing anything new. Those three cost about an hour between them and every other row on this board is more valuable after them.
