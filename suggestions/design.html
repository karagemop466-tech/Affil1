Copy-ready design prompt

Design Crisp & Counter as an independent kitchen buying publication. Use warm paper, dark green accents, legible serif headlines, and clear food photography or visibly labelled illustrations. On mobile, start with one useful choice: single basket, dual basket, or oven. On each card, write a cooking scenario, one trade-off, and one thing to check next. Make the comparison readable by keyboard and on small screens. Give guides a consistent editorial byline, sources, and related reading. Put the Associates disclosure under the masthead and in the footer; keep the source register and decision tools one click away. Never show an invented test result, star rating, sale, or ranking. Do not show a tracked shopping CTA until the destination and attribution are verified.

A cadence that can respond to results

  1. Weekly: Check indexing and query/page patterns in the verified Search Console property. Log the date range and missing data. The first themes are small kitchens, meal prep, holiday sides, and food thermometers, not proven search trends.
  2. Every other week: Publish one distinct question or improve an existing guide. Update an existing page rather than creating a near-duplicate. The build adds each accepted guide to the sitemap and RSS feed.
  3. Every 28 days: Review query and landing-page data against the previous comparable window. Keep on-site outbound counts separate from Associates orders. If the data are sparse, improve the guide or keep collecting; don’t estimate demand, commissions, or conversion.

Hosting gate: GitHub Pages usage limits restricts using Pages for sites primarily intended to facilitate commercial transactions. Treat this Pages release as editorial and move the monetized operation to appropriate hosting if it becomes primarily commercial.
