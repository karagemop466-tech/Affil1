Automated Pre-Publish Gate Checklist

  • Operating Agreement §5 verbatim disclosure: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” appears above <main> and in <footer> on every published page.
  • Outbound paid-link qualification: Every Amazon destination on these pages is an /dp/ listing and carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener". Regulatory and technical citations carry rel="noopener" only.
  • API-gated commercial fields: Zero static prices, star ratings, customer review excerpts/counts, stock claims, or price-alert tools appear on static pages (Program Policies §2(b), §6(t), §6(y)).
  • Identifier separation: intended_asin and observed_asin are stored in distinct schema objects and never merged (S03).
