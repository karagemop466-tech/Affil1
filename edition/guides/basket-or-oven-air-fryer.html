Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Pull-out drawers move hot air fast around loose pieces you shake; French-door countertop ovens hold flat sheet trays, toast slices, and shallow baking pans. Compare cavity shape before preset counts.

Prompt Core Hypothesis #1 — working editorial hypothesis, not a popularity or lab-test claim

Start with how food sits, not how many buttons light up

Every air fryer on this shortlist circulates heated air with a fan, yet the three air-fryer listings observed on Amazon divide into two completely different physical shapes. P-01 (CHEFMAN 6-Quart Dual Basket) and P-03 (Chefman TurboFry 8-Quart) are pull-out drawer machines: food rests on a perforated insert inside closed walls. P-02 (Emeril Lagasse 26-Quart French Door) is a countertop convection toaster oven with outward-swinging doors and slide-in racks.

If most of your weeknight cooking is cut potatoes, broccoli florets, chicken thighs, dumplings, or frozen snacks that benefit from a mid-cook toss, a pull-out basket lets you grip one handle, shake, and slide the drawer straight back in. If you routinely slide in a quarter-sheet tray, toast four slices of sourdough, or bake in a shallow dish, a rack cavity handles flat cookware that cannot enter a narrow drawer.

Door swing vs drawer pull: measure the front of your counter

A basket machine needs straight pull-out clearance directly in front of the housing plus a heat-resistant spot to set a hot drawer when you turn food with tongs. A French-door oven opens two doors outward into the workspace in front of the unit while hot racks slide forward.

Before clicking through to any listing, measure three zones on your own counter: under-cabinet height (including steam vents), counter depth from backsplash to front lip, and landing space beside the appliance for hot pans.

Preset buttons rarely change the underlying hardware

Seller titles highlight preset counts — four functions on P-03, sync-finish and hi-fry on P-01, twenty-four one-touch presets on P-02. Treat presets as time-and-temperature shortcuts rather than distinct cooking engines. What actually separates daily use is whether you have one wide drawer (P-03), two independent drawers (P-01), or multi-rack oven shelves (P-02).
