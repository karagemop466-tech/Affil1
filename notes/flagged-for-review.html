Executive summary

What this is: a publication-style storefront with seven question-first buyer guides, a five-item submitted-link register, decision-support pages (suggestions queue, decision questions, executive summary) and this review queue. Selected tracking ID supplied: shopnow047c-20; enrollment and per-link tracking attribution are not yet confirmed in the enrolled Associates workflow. What is confirmed (20 Nov 2026 verification pass): four of five submitted short links resolved live to official Amazon product pages, with titles taken from the pages themselves; the fifth consistently lands on Amazon's automated checkpoint page. What is not confirmed: per-link tracking attribution for tag shopnow047c-20, current prices, ratings, review counts, commission rates, and any sales data. No product winner, conversion score or revenue prediction is published anywhere on this site.

Flagged irregularities

  • FLG-001 All five submitted slugs are 9 characters. Amazon ASINs are 10-character identifiers (see the SP-API Catalog Items reference, which defines the asin path parameter). The slugs are therefore treated as opaque short-link tokens; ASINs are read only from resolved listing URLs.
  • FLG-002 No supplied link was observed to carry shopnow047c-20. The storefront's outbound links append the tag per the Associates Special Link format, but per-link tracking confirmation requires the enrolled Associates workflow (account access not available to this build).
  • FLG-003 The compliance status of link.amazon short links as Associates "Special Links" is unresolved. Amazon owns the .amazon gTLD and the links resolve, but no official statement equates them with SiteStripe-generated Special Links. Do not assert invalid; do not scale until one link per product is tested in the enrolled workflow.
  • FLG-004 Commission rates for the relevant categories are behind Associates sign-in; omitted rather than guessed.
  • FLG-005 One short link (B0c8moW59) was checkpoint-gated on this session’s repeated fetches; a parallel session later reached dp/B0DHZTCNR5 (ThermoMaven 1 Sec Digital Meat Thermometer) through it, and this session confirmed that page live. Identity mapping remains disputed.
  • FLG-016 Verification sessions disagree on the intended mapping of P-04 (B0c8moW59) and P-05 (B0f3GKalj): this session and the PR #27 session both observed P-05 → dp/B0DZFD6R9Z (CHEF iQ Sense), while another session reported the destinations reversed. All observations are preserved in the register; settle the mapping in the enrolled Associates workflow before promoting either listing.
  • FLG-006 No price, rating, review count or availability is published: Associates policy conditions price display on Amazon-served or API-served data, which this static build does not have. Cards show identity and verification status only.
  • FLG-007 Product attributes beyond the official listing titles (dimensions, wattage, warranty) are unverified; guides deliberately reference "the official manual" instead of quoting numbers.
  • FLG-008 Editorial titles are hypotheses. No search-volume or popularity data was obtained; seasonal framing is editorial.
  • FLG-009 The analytics endpoint is specified (assets/js/analytics.js, server/collect.js, analytics/endpoint.py) but not deployed; events persist to localStorage and the beacon fails silently until the operator self-hosts a collector.
  • FLG-010 The campaign ledger is intentionally empty. No conversions, revenue or forecasts are claimed.
  • FLG-011 Search Console verification and sitemap submission require account access; not performed. Sitemap and feeds are published and correct for submission.
  • FLG-012 Reader email capture requires a provider and a privacy review; not performed.

Disclosure and links

The Associates Operating Agreement asks Associates to clearly and prominently state: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” This exact statement appears above the masthead and in the footer of every page. FTC guidance says affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, close to the recommendation or link. This page is not legal advice.

Suggestions queue — choose after evidence

  1. Highest priority — confirm tracking: test one link per product in the enrolled Associates workflow (SiteStripe), confirm destination and tracking report, then scale. The register's observed targets are recorded and separated.
  2. Reader trust — factual comparisons: with identities confirmed, compare only sourced attributes (format, capacity, cleanup, dimensions) and leave unknown cells blank.
  3. Search discovery — publish on cadence: keep the question-led guides coming (one per two weeks proposed), update sitemap/feed on each publish, review measured queries monthly before choosing the next topic.
  4. Measurement — deploy after authorization: instrument the five defined events (page_view, outbound_click, guide_read, suggestion_review, session_start) via the self-hosted collector after data-access and privacy decisions.
  5. Operations — gate content: before each publish: disclosure visible, sponsored rel attributes present, source provenance recorded, Amazon API/policy review, endorsement context reviewed.

Recommendation: begin with tracking confirmation and disclosure/policy review, not automated product scoring. No revenue or conversion forecasts are made.
