The desk · Product records

The product-intent
confirmation record.

For every submitted link: the intended ASIN, the official listing URL, the confirmation date and the source — kept in their own fields, and never merged with the redirect target that was actually observed.

Why two fields and not one

An Amazon Standard Identification Number is a unique 10-character alphanumeric code that appears after /dp/ in a product detail URL. Each of the five submitted tokens is nine characters long, so none of them is an ASIN. Keeping the intent and the observation apart means a redirect can never quietly become a claim.

FLG-002

Reading these records correctly

An observed redirect is evidence that a link resolved to a listing on a given date. It is not evidence that the product is the one intended, that the link carries a valid tag, or that it will earn a commission. Those are separate questions, and they are recorded separately.
