What is collected by the tracker

Event name, page path, referrer host, the outbound host a reader clicked, a guide id, a timestamp, and a truncated salted hash of the IP address that changes daily. No cookies, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting, no cross-site identifiers, and no storage of query strings on outbound links. Retention is 90 days.
