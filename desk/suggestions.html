The desk · Suggestions

Suggestions,
with a decision slot.

Fifteen proposals, each with the evidence behind it, what it depends on, how much effort it takes, and what could go wrong. Nothing here has been applied automatically. The last column is deliberately empty so a reviewer can choose.

How to use this page

Read the group headings in order. The Now group is what should happen before the site is scaled or a product is written about at length. The Next group is about how the site reads and looks. The Later group is about measurement and automation, and it carries the highest risk items. Write a decision in the last column, or on a copy, and it becomes the build order.
