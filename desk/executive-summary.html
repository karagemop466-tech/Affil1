The desk · Executive summary

The position,
in one page.

What was confirmed, what was found, what is still open, and the five decisions that would move this forward.

Situation

Five Amazon short links were supplied to market. Following each of them over HTTPS on 20 November 2026 resolved four of them to specific Amazon product detail pages and sent the fifth to an Amazon checkpoint page. The five link tokens are nine characters long, and an Amazon Standard Identification Number is ten characters, so none of the supplied tokens is an ASIN. The site publishes canonical product links carrying the supplied tracking ID, and records the intent and the observation in separate fields.

What is already done

  • Every published product link is a canonical Amazon product link carrying the supplied tracking ID, and is marked as a sponsored placement.
  • The Associates disclosure sits above the masthead on every page and is repeated in the footer.
  • Six question-shaped guides are published, each with a byline, dates, a cluster label and a note naming what is still unconfirmed.
  • A product-intent confirmation record keeps the intended listing, the intended ASIN, the confirmation date and the source separate from the observed redirect target.
  • A suggestions register, an open-questions register, a publishing gate and a flagged-for-review page are published for review.
  • The build is automated: site pages are rendered from one data file, and scripts/check_site.py gates the result before deployment.

Findings

Five things the check turned up.

Each links to the official source it was read from.

Four destinations confirmed, one blocked

CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven (B0CDY277D3), Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Multi-Function Air Fryer Toaster Oven (B09B7SB46R), Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1 (B08DKYBTPH) and CHEF iQ Sense Gen3 wireless meat thermometer (B0DZFD6R9Z). The fifth short link returned an Amazon “Continue shopping” checkpoint and was not observed.

Source: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CDY277D3

One link is not an air fryer

The link in slot 5 resolves to a wireless meat thermometer whose listing names air fryer among its uses. It is filed under Cooking tools and flagged as a scope question rather than presented as a cooker.

Source: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DZFD6R9Z

Tracking could not be confirmed, and that is a finding

A control fetch of a URL that carried the supplied tracking ID returned a resolved URL with no tag parameter, which proves the fetch path used for this site normalises the tag away. So neither the presence nor the absence of a tag on the supplied links can be observed this way. The links are not called invalid; the question is open.

Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement

Prices and ratings are deliberately absent from the shop

Three prices were observed on 20 November 2026 and are recorded on the product records page as dated observations. Ratings could not be read at all, because Amazon's review pages redirect to sign-in. The reader-facing pages show no price and no rating, because the Associates IP License permits Product Advertising Content only through Creators API or PA API.

Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

The measurement layer ships, deployed, honest

A privacy-preserving tracker and a zero-dependency collector are in the repository. No endpoint is deployed, no report has been supplied, and the campaign ledger is empty. No click count, order count, conversion rate or revenue figure appears anywhere on this site.

Source: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9128668
