About this publication

Clear sources. Honest limits.

Countertop Notes is designed to help people make a kitchen purchase decision, not to disguise a product spreadsheet as editorial advice.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some outgoing product links may earn commissions if qualifying purchases are made. The disclosure appears at the top of every page and again in the footer; individual product links are labeled “affiliate.” The presence of a link does not mean a product has been independently tested or endorsed.

The FTC’s endorsement guidance discusses clear and conspicuous disclosure of affiliate relationships near recommendations and links. Review current source material and the context of every page before publication.

What we verify

  • Product name, model, ASIN and official listing must match the intended item; missing values stay blank.
  • The intended item and a link’s observed redirect destination are stored separately.
  • Product facts are only added with an exact-model official source and a dated provenance note.
  • Prices and availability are not shown here. Amazon policies restrict when and how those may be displayed; see the current Program Policies.
  • We do not invent popularity, search volume, review data, conversions, commission rates, or personal testing.

What is not yet verified

The five URLs supplied for this project have not been matched to intended products, tested in the enrolled Associates workflow, or confirmed for destination and tracking attribution. The selected ID was supplied as shopnow047c-20; account enrollment and association are unconfirmed. The site register and Flagged for review page show the open items.

Images and editorial limits

The hero is a generic AI-generated kitchen still life, not an image of any promoted product and not a product photograph. The four guides are original decision checklists, not test reports. They describe ways a reader can compare candidates and deliberately avoid product-specific performance claims.

Measurement and privacy

The browser code recognizes five event names, but events are not sent unless a self-hosted endpoint is configured. GitHub Pages serves static files; it does not execute the server implementation included in this repository. The standalone endpoint is designed to store an allowlisted event, sanitized page path, optional internal item code, and server receive time, without storing IP address, user agent, referrer, cookies, raw query strings, or client timestamps. Hosting-provider logs and local law/policy still require review before deployment.
