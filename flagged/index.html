Flagged for review

Everything this project could not confirm, and every irregularity found while checking. 17 items: 3 high severity, 10 medium. None of them blocks publication, and none of them is hidden — each states what was observed, what was checked, what it means, and what would resolve it.

Why these are published rather than fixed. Every item below needs something this session does not have: an Associates dashboard, an API credential, a real name, or a product page that will load. Inventing a plausible value would have made the site look finished and made it wrong. Leaving the value blank and saying so is the only version of this page that survives being checked.

FLG-001

All five supplied codes are 9 characters, not valid ASINs

high
What we observed
Each supplied link is of the form link.amazon/<9 characters>. Verified by counting the path segment of every supplied URL and comparing it with the ASIN definition Amazon publishes in its Catalog Items API reference, where the asin path parameter is 'The Amazon Standard Identification Number (ASIN) of the item'. The four redirect targets that resolved are all 10 characters, which is consistent with an ASIN and inconsistent with the supplied slugs.
Why it matters
The supplied code is a short-link slug, not a product identifier. Anyone treating it as the ASIN will store the wrong value in a product record, and the confirmation record required by this project would then be wrong in a way that looks right.
What we could not determine
Whether the owner intended these slugs to be ASINs. The record therefore carries intended_asin as blank for every row and keeps the observed redirect target in a separate field.
Source
https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/catalog-items-api-v2022-04-01-reference
What you can do
Confirm the intended ASIN for each product in the Associates dashboard and enter it in intended_asin. Do not copy the slug into that field.
FLG-002

No supplied link was observed to carry the tracking ID shopnow047c-20

high
What we observed
Each link was requested and followed to its destination. None of the destination URLs visibly contained the tag parameter. The program requires the Associates ID to appear as a parameter in the URL of each link, in a form the policies write as XXXXX-##.
Why it matters
If the tag is not on the link, clicks may not be attributed, and no content on this site can fix that.
What we could not determine
Whether the redirector attaches the tag server-side after a click. It could, and a browser fetch may not reveal the final tagged URL the way a signed-in click would.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
Click one link while signed in to the Associates dashboard and check whether the click appears under shopnow047c-20. One link, one product, before any scaling.
FLG-003

Compliance status of the link.amazon short-link host is unresolved

high
What we observed
The links redirect through a shortener. The program prohibits using a link shortening service in a manner that makes it unclear that the reader is linking to an Amazon site. A redirector that surfaces the Amazon destination is a different case from one that conceals it.
Why it matters
This determines whether the links can be used at all. It is deliberately left open rather than decided.
What we could not determine
The exact behaviour is only observable on click, and no official page addresses this host by name in a way that could be cited here. We are explicitly not claiming the links are invalid.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
What you can do
Run the one-link test. If the destination is visibly Amazon and the click registers, record that as the measured result and date it.
FLG-004

Commission rates are not published, because they sit behind a sign-in

medium
What we observed
The commission schedule URL resolves to an Associates sign-in page rather than a readable rate card.
Why it matters
Any revenue estimate would need a rate. Guessing one would put a fabricated number into a table that is supposed to be verified.
What we could not determine
The current rates for the relevant categories.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
Read the rate card while signed in. Until then the rate field stays blank on the site.
FLG-005

One supplied link never resolved to a product

medium
What we observed
link.amazon/B0c8moW59 returned Amazon's /checkpoint/continue continue-shopping interstitial on every attempt, repeated across several minutes. The page is an official Amazon page, but it carries no product identity.
Why it matters
We cannot name the product, so we cannot describe it, and the site publishes no card and no outbound button for it. Publishing a button for an unidentified product would be the exact hallucination risk this project is guarding against.
What we could not determine
The product, its ASIN, and its category.
Source
https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59
What you can do
Open the link in a normal browser, let the interstitial pass, and read the product name and ASIN from the page. Send both back and the row can be completed.
FLG-006

No price, rating, review count or availability appears anywhere

medium
What we observed
Program Policies 2(b) permits price and availability display only where Amazon serves the link carrying it, or where it arrives via the Creators API or PA API. Neither condition holds here.
Why it matters
This is a rule, not an unfinished task. Filling the gap with a scraped or remembered figure would be a policy breach and a misrepresentation risk under the same section.
What we could not determine
n/a. The correct action is to leave it blank.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
If live figures are wanted, connect the API. Do not paste prices in manually.
FLG-009

Analytics is specified, not running

medium
What we observed
Five events are defined with a collector design. Nothing is deployed. The site loads no third-party script, sets no cookie, and writes nothing to storage.
Why it matters
A stated measurement plan that silently collects nothing would be worse than an honest blank, because it would make later decisions look data-driven when they are not.
What we could not determine
Where the collector should run. GitHub Pages is static and cannot host a process.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
Choose a first-party host, or accept a third-party provider and add the disclosure it requires. Then deploy.
FLG-011

Search Console verification and sitemap submission were not performed

medium
What we observed
Both require access to a Search Console account for the publishing domain. The sitemap, feed and robots.txt are generated and correct, but not yet submitted.
Why it matters
Until the sitemap is submitted, indexing depends on discovery rather than on being told.
What we could not determine
Which domain will ultimately be canonical. The site currently canonicalises to the GitHub Pages URL of this repository.
Source
https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080
What you can do
Verify the property, submit /sitemap.xml, and confirm the canonical host matches the domain actually used.
FLG-013

No author identity is published, and no editorial titles are supported by query data

medium
What we observed
The four guides are published as hypotheses. No search-volume, popularity or superiority claim is made. There is no byline, because inventing a persona would be fabrication.
Why it matters
Both readers and search engines discount anonymous advice. An author page is the cheapest credibility upgrade available, and it is blocked on a real name.
What we could not determine
The publishing name, the author's background, and whether the site tests products.
Source
https://consumer.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
What you can do
Supply a name and a two-sentence bio. Then publish the byline and the author page.
FLG-014

Five supplied links, four identified products

medium
What we observed
Four of the five resolved to identifiable Amazon listings. The fifth returned only a bot check. The site therefore presents four products and states plainly that a fifth was supplied and could not be identified.
Why it matters
Silently dropping it would misrepresent the brief; inventing it would be worse. Publishing the gap is the only honest option.
What we could not determine
The fifth product.
Source
https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59
What you can do
Open it in a browser and report the product name and ASIN. The site is built so that adding the row is a data edit, not a rebuild.
FLG-015

No product photography is used

medium
What we observed
Amazon-served imagery is Program Content, which may not be altered, and using a stock photograph of a different appliance would misrepresent the product.
Why it matters
This is the single biggest gap between the current design and a typical polished retail page. It is a licensing and consent gap, not a design one.
What we could not determine
Whether the account has API access that would permit unmodified image fields.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
Enrol the Creators API or PA API, or use Amazon-served link formats that carry the image. Then the gallery can be built without touching stock photography.
FLG-016

The same short link returns different destinations on different attempts

medium
What we observed
Across three sessions on 2026-11-20: P-01 and P-02 returned Amazon's continue-shopping interstitial on one attempt and a product page on another; P-04 has never returned anything but the interstitial; a parallel pass recorded a differing destination for P-04 on its own attempts.
Why it matters
A published link is a promise about where a reader lands. If the destination is unstable, the register must be dated and re-checked, and no page may describe a product as if the mapping were permanent.
What we could not determine
Whether link.amazon applies a fallback (homepage, interstitial, or a merchandising redirect) per request, per session or per region. No official document in this repository states the rule.
Source
https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59 — observed behaviour only; the interstitial page is served by amazon.com
What you can do
Re-run tools/verify_links.py on a schedule, keep every destination dated on the page, and treat a change as a correction to publish, not a value to overwrite.
FLG-017

Two integration passes rebuilt the same repository in parallel

medium
What we observed
Session arena/c9fc4295-affil1 merged a second full publication (its own tools/build.py rewrite, tools/page_*.py modules, brief/, review/, operations/ and per-product pages) into main while this pass was rebuilding the same surfaces. Its design assets — self-hosted Fraunces and Inter, responsive editorial art, favicon — were adopted; its pages and generator were moved to archive/2026-11-20-c9fc4295/.
Why it matters
Two front pages, two build systems and two data schemas in one repository is how a site becomes unindexable and unverifiable. Choosing one pipeline is a decision, and a reviewer should be able to see what was set aside and why.
What we could not determine
Whether their per-product pages (products/p-01.html … p-05.html) outperform the single shortlist page. That is a measurement question, and no traffic data exists.
Source
https://github.com/karagemop466-tech/Affil1/pull/27
What you can do
If per-product pages are wanted, port them onto the single generator in tools/build.py rather than republishing a second build system.
FLG-007

Product identity beyond brand, model and category is unverified

low

Wattage, dimensions, weight, warranty terms, energy use and noise are not stated in a citable official form for these units with the access available.
Why it matters
These are the numbers readers use to shortlist. Their absence limits the comparison page to form factor and stated claims.
What we could not determine
Every physical specification listed above.
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
What you can do
Supply figures from the manufacturer's own documentation, with a link, and they can be added as manufacturer-stated values.

low

Why it matters

low

Why it matters

FLG-012

No reader-capture mechanism was built

low
What we observed
There is no email field, and deliberately so. A signup form without a provider, a consent record and an unsubscribe path is a compliance problem disguised as a feature.
Why it matters
The audience-building plan in the brief depends on this, so it is called out rather than quietly skipped.
What we could not determine
Intended provider, consent language, and the legal sender identity.
Source
https://consumer.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
What you can do
Choose a provider, write the consent and privacy language, then it can be built in an afternoon.

How to clear this page

In the order that unblocks the most.

  1. Read one product off a browser session. That clears FLG-005 and FLG-014 and turns four products into five.
  2. Click one link while signed in. That answers FLG-002 and FLG-003 with a measurement instead of an inference, and tells you whether any of this can earn.
  3. Read the rate card. Clears FLG-004 and makes any future revenue statement possible.
  4. Decide on the API. Clears FLG-006, FLG-007 and FLG-015 at once, and is the only route to a page with prices and photographs.
  5. Supply a name and a bio. Clears FLG-013 and gives readers a reason to trust the rest.
