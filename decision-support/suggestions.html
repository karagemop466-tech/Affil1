Start with Path A

Because it is the only path whose outputs open the other two: an unnamed product, a disputed link and an unconfirmed tag are the three reasons the shop cannot honestly sell or measure anything. Three coherent paths

Each path is complete on its own terms. Mixing them is fine; running C before A is the one combination that produces numbers nobody can defend.

Path A · Trust the shelf

Make the five products real, then monetisable

  • Resolve the register contradiction and pick one authoritative file
  • Confirm the tracking ID's enrolment and registered Site
  • Run the single-product Special Link test, record it, then scale to five
  • Drop or fix the unnamed product

Roughly 6–8 focused hours. Unblocks: honest outbound links, the commission question, and every measurement claim.

Deliberately not suggested

Six things we will not talk you into

Each of these is a standard growth move that this project's own evidence rules out. They belong on the board as rejections, so nobody re-proposes them in the next session.

Scraped prices "for now"

Rejected. Price data may only come from an Amazon-served link or the APIs, and we have neither. A snapshot price is a misleading claim with a decimal point in it. A-09, A-11

Buying the first three sales

Rejected, and it would end the account. Personal orders do not qualify, and a termination for abuse cannot be rejoined without Amazon's advance authorisation. A-17, A-31

Paid ads straight to Amazon

Rejected. Purchases referred through any advertisement you purchased are disqualified outright — and bidding on "amazon" keywords is named as its own exclusion. A-22

Mass-generating 200 AI pages

Rejected. It is the definition of thin affiliation and scaled content abuse, and it would take the whole domain with it. A measured cadence is the plan: editorial plan. G-01, G-05

Auto-redirect wrappers on outbound links

Rejected. A link that sends the reader onward without an affirmative click is a "Redirecting Link" and earns nothing. A-24

Per-user sub-tag profiling

Rejected as a policy wall, not a preference: no Associates ID or sub-tag may be tied to a specific end user. A-08
