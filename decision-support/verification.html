How to read the status column

verbatim quoted word-for-word from the official page, read this session.

official · numeric a number taken from an official page snapshot; listings change, so it is a date-stamped reading, not a fact about today.

inference our conclusion drawn from a verified rule. Labelled, never hidden inside a sentence that reads like policy.

unresolved nobody could confirm it — not Amazon, not this workspace. It stays blank.

A cell that is blank and flagged is worth more than a cell that is full and guessed. That is the whole rule of this page.
