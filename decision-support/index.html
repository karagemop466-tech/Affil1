State of play

Three bolts are missing

Everything else in this project is craftsmanship. These three are facts only an account holder can supply — which is why an agent session could describe them precisely and could not close them.

Bolt 1 · identity

Nobody has confirmed where the five links go

Each submitted string is nine characters, where Amazon publishes ASINs as ten. Four candidate listings are confirmed real. A same-day audit observed destinations for all five links and aligned the two registers that had contradicted each other here — but every one of those observations came from someone else's sandbox, and the enrolled workflow has reproduced none of them.

How this desk was made

Method, stated plainly

So that a later session can repeat it, and so that the parts a model could not reach are visible rather than smoothed over.

1 · Official text only

Policy claims quote the page Amazon, the FTC or Google serves today, with the document's own revision date. Blogs and legal summaries are used as leads, never as evidence, and say so when cited.

2 · Blank over inferred

Prices seen in snapshots were discarded rather than published. The commission rate, product availability and review counts stay empty because the permitted source for them does not exist yet.

3 · Contradiction, published

Where two registers on this branch disagree, both readings are printed side by side with a recommendation for resolving them. Averaging them would have been the confident, wrong answer.

4 · Limits disclosed

This workspace's egress is restricted to an allow-list, so amazon.com could not be fetched at all here. The DNS lookup succeeded; the redirect chain was never seen. That is recorded in the register, not hidden.
