Read this first · ten minutes, no jargon

A shop with good bones
and three missing bolts.

The brief asked for an automated, compliant, traffic-winning Amazon affiliate platform. What exists today is a hand-written editorial site with an unusually honest evidence trail, five product links nobody has confirmed, and an automation layer that policy will not let us build yet. This page states the distance between those three things — and what closes it.

The one-line version

Confirm the links, name the author, pick the publisher

Everything else in this project is downstream of three confirmations that together take about an hour of a person's time inside the Associates dashboard. No model can substitute for them, because they are account facts, not research questions.

The part with numbers in it

Honest economics

Every figure here is a term, not a forecast. There is deliberately no revenue projection in this project, because there is no report data and no API access to build one from.

What a commission is

A percentage of Qualifying Revenue, which Amazon defines as what it actually receives for the purchase less shipping, gift-wrapping, handling, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, card-processing fees and bad debt. The rate itself lives in the Commission Income Statement Appendix; the number for these categories was not readable without an account, so it is blank here — not estimated.

A-21 · A-33 (unresolved)

How long you have

24 hours from the click for the session to matter, 89 days for a carted item to be ordered, 180 days for it to ship and be paid. A reader who clicks at midnight and buys on Saturday has bought nothing for us.

A-19, A-20

The four gates ahead of us

  • 3 qualifying sales in 180 days — or the application is withdrawn, and a rejected ID cannot be reinstated.
  • 10 qualifying sales in the last 30 days — the eligibility floor for Creators API / PA API access.
  • ≥10 posts of robust original content — Amazon's own rule of thumb at review.
  • Site named in the application — links on an unlisted property are not permitted.

A-31, A-32, A-29, A-15

What is paid, and when

About 60 days after the month's end. Minimum payout: 10 USD by direct deposit, 10 USD by gift card (2,000 USD cap), 100 USD by cheque with a 15 USD processing fee. An account idle for three years can have accrued income withheld on seven days' notice.

A-27

The consequence for the roadmap. The brief's headline ambition — an agent that researches products, reads prices and reviews, and optimises campaigns automatically — is gated behind the ten-sales-in-thirty-days threshold, and price display is gated behind the same API. So the only serious sequence is: publish something a person would read → earn three real sales → keep ten in a rolling month → then automate. The gates are not obstacles around the business; they are the business's entry requirements.

What could go wrong

Risks, ranked by what they cost

Ranked by consequence, not likelihood. The top two are recoverable only by losing the account, which is why they sit above the traffic questions.

Existential

Tagged links on an unconfirmed account

Live pages already carry tag=shopnow047c-20. If that account is not approved for this URL, the links are Special Links on a site that wasn't named in the application — and §2 lets Amazon withhold all commission income, not just the affected ones.

Mitigation: confirm, or ship untagged until confirmed. An hour of checking. Q-02

Existential

Account closure at the 180-day mark

The account either produces three qualifying sales or is withdrawn, and a rejected ID cannot be reinstated. A site with no traffic and no confirmed products has no route to three sales.

Mitigation: the front page, the guide cadence and the disclosure work is the compliance mitigation. Path B

Reputational

A shop that reads like a form

Duplicated sections, internal task codes in navigation, no author, no prices, and caveats above the fold. Each is individually defensible and collectively fatal to trust — and to the "not AI slop" instruction in the brief.

Mitigation: one brand, one nav, real bylines, product-first pages. Q-08, Q-10

Strategic

Optimising what cannot be measured

Three analytics modules, no receiver, and a host that cannot run one. Any dashboard built now would report the emptiness of a bucket.

Mitigation: one contract, or a stated local-only ledger. Q-07

Strategic

Duplicate content teaching search engines to ignore us

Four ways to reach "products", three ways to reach a feed, canonicals on half the pages. Not a penalty waiting to happen so much as a site with no single identity to rank.

Mitigation: canonical set + 301s + one sitemap. IRR-07

Operational

Automation drift on live pages

A promotion that ends and stays mentioned; a listing that changes and the page that doesn't. Both are named in policy as the publisher's problem.

Mitigation: dated provenance on every claim, and an expires_at field enforced by the build. Suggestions S-08, S-09

What "good" looks like in a month

One site, five confirmed-and-tagged products, twelve planned guides with two published, a working query feed, and a ledger that is still empty but whose columns are provably fed by an authorised report.

What we are not claiming

No ranking. No conversion rate. No revenue. No "best air fryer". No statement that the short links are invalid — only that their compliance status is unresolved in the brief and remains so after this review.

What to decide today

Three things: whether the tag stays until enrolment is confirmed, which section is the shop, and whose name goes on the articles. Every other row in this desk follows from those answers.
