The distinction that matters

Missing ≠ unknowable

Most gaps here are not research problems. They are access problems: an Associates dashboard, an API key, a Search Console property, a person who owns the kitchen appliance. A model can find nothing that only an account can show.

7 of the 14 are answerable only by whoever holds the Associates account — and about an hour in that dashboard closes them. That is the highest-leverage hour in this project.

Verified this session

Nine answers we can already give you

These were open in the brief. They are now settled against official pages, so nobody needs to re-litigate them at the next session.

What counts as an ASIN?

Settled · Amazon's own definition

A 10-character alphanumeric identifier, usually beginning "B0", shown in a product page URL after /dp/. All five submitted strings are 9 characters, so they are short-link slugs — and that is a format observation, not a verdict that the links are broken.

Exactly what must the disclosure say, and where?

Settled · Operating Agreement §5 + 16 CFR §255.0(f)

The sentence "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." must be clear and prominent wherever Program Content appears. The FTC adds the placement standard: difficult to miss, easily understandable, and on the internet, unavoidable. A strip above the masthead plus a repeat in the footer satisfies both; a footer alone does not satisfy "unavoidable".

May the site show prices?

Settled · Program Policies §2(b)

Only where Amazon serves the link containing the price, or where pricing comes from the Creators API / PA API under its licence. Everything else is a breach — which is why the shop has no prices, not even ones that look current.

Can we attribute clicks to individual readers with rotating sub-tags?

Settled · Program Policies §2(b)

No. "Under no circumstances" may a sub-tag or any Associates reporting be associated with a specific end user, including dynamically assigned per visit. Any personalisation design that needs per-user attribution is dead on arrival, and that is a rule, not a risk appetite.

How long is the attribution window?

Settled · Commission Income Statement §1

The session starts at the click and ends at whichever comes first: 24 hours, the customer ordering any non-digital product, or the customer clicking another Associate's link. A product added to the cart inside that session can still pay up to 89 days later, and the order must ship and be paid for within 180 days of purchase.

Is commission a share of the price the reader sees?

Settled · Commission Income Statement §1

No. Qualifying Revenue is what Amazon actually receives for that purchase, less shipping, gift-wrapping, handling, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, card-processing fees and bad debt. Any revenue model built on sticker price overstates earnings before it starts.

Can we buy traffic to these links?

Settled · §2(c), (d), (e)

No. Purchases from any advertisement you paid for are disqualified, as are referrals from links displayed on a search engine and "Redirecting Links" that forward the reader without an affirmative click. Three of the standard growth tactics for this business model are excluded by name.

When can the automation actually start?

Settled · Creators API prerequisites

Not on command: API access needs ten qualifying sales within the past thirty days. So the pipeline order is fixed by policy — publish, earn three real sales to be reviewed at all, earn ten in a month to read product data programmatically. Anything promising "automated optimisation" before that is automating blind.

What does a thin affiliate site look like to Google, and what does a good one do?

Settled · Google spam policies

Thin affiliation is affiliate content where descriptions and reviews are copied from the merchant with no original value. Google names the antidote: additional price information, original reviews, real testing and ratings, clear navigation of categories, and comparisons. Three of those five need something we do not have yet — which is the whole argument for Q-13 and Q-04.

How to use this list. Answer Q-01, Q-02 and Q-03 in a single sitting: six of these fourteen carry a launch-blocking severity, those three close themselves, and Q-11 shrinks to one email to Associates support. Q-07, Q-08 and Q-14 are decisions no amount of fetching resolves. The rest are decisions, not research — and a decision with no answer in it is still a decision, which is what the suggestion board is for.
