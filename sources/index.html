How to re-check this page in ten minutes

  1. Open the Operating Agreement and search for "I earn from qualifying purchases". It is section 5.
  2. Open the Program Policies, find Participation Requirements 2(b), and read the sentence about the tag parameter.
  3. Open the FTC FAQ and find "A disclosure should be placed where it easily catches consumers' attention".
  4. Open any product page on this site, take the ASIN, and open amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>. If the titles match, the register is right.
  5. If a check fails, note the date. Prices, titles and policies all change; a source is a snapshot, not a permanent fact.
