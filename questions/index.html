Five ways to find the missing information

Each of these produces a fact this site currently does not have. All five are free, and four of them are faster than writing another article.

  1. Ask Amazon in writing. Whether the short links are Special Links, what a valid tag looks like, and whether the ID is active, are three answerable questions for the programme's own support channel.
  2. Verify Search Console and wait 28 days. Impressions by query is the only honest substitute for the volume data this site does not have.
  3. Watch one click land in the report. A single tagged click settles whether the machinery works end to end.
  4. Ask five readers one question: "what did you nearly buy, and what stopped you?" Five answers beat any keyword tool for knowing which guide to write next.
  5. Read the manual for one product. Dimensions, wattage and care instructions are the facts most often missing from a listing title, and the manufacturer publishes them.
