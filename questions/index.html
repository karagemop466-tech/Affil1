Q-01Open Blocks work

Did each submitted link land on the product the owner meant to send readers to?

What we have
All five links now land on a live listing, and the arrival ASIN and title were transcribed on 2026-11-20: B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5 and B0DZFD6R9Z.
What is missing
The owner's intended ASIN. The supplied codes are nine characters and lowercase; every ASIN observed is ten and uppercase. The intended product cannot be read off the link itself.

What would close it

In the Associates dashboard, open SiteStripe for each product and copy the tagged link for the exact ASIN intended. Record both values side by side, as the register does.

Q-03Open Blocks work

Are clicks attributed to shopnow047c-20?

What we have
No Amazon link on the site carries ?tag=shopnow047c-20 yet. Program Policies 2(a)-(b) require a Special Link to use the Associates ID; a Special Link generated in Associates Central and validated by one test click has not been recorded.
What is missing
Confirmation from an Associates report that a click was recorded against the tag. No dashboard access is available to this build.

What would close it

Click one link on the live site, then open Reports → Orders in the Associates dashboard and look for the click against the tag.

Q-10Open Blocks work

Is there an analytics endpoint that can be switched on lawfully?

What we have
A self-hosted collector (_analytics/endpoint.py) and a cookie-free client that records five defined events. Both are inert.
What is missing
A host for the collector, a retention statement, and a lawful basis for processing IP addresses.

What would close it

Deploy the collector to a host you control, set retention, edit the privacy page with the specifics, then point the client at it.

Q-14Open Blocks work

Are the product links Special Links in the sense the Operating Agreement means?

What we have
Program Policies 2(a) requires Special Links to use the Associates ID; 2(b) requires the tag as a URL parameter. No product link carries the tag yet; buy buttons are plain listing links, marked sponsored and labelled as listing sources.
What is missing
Confirmation that the tag is registered and that clicks from this site are being recorded. That can only come from the account.

What would close it

One click from the live site, then the Orders report. The same test closes Q-03.

Q-08Open

Who is the author, and why should a reader trust them?

What we have
No named author. The site's claim is narrow and checkable: the links were opened and the listings read.

Name an editor and write an accurate bio, or keep the publication anonymous and stop implying personal expertise. Both are honest; the second is weaker for search.

Q-24Open

Q-04Answered

Should the site show prices?

What we have
Program Policies 2(b) permits prices and availability only where Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or where it arrives via Creators API or PA API (SRC-02). Neither is connected.
What is missing
A decision, and if the answer is yes, API credentials.

Answer

No price, star rating, review count or stock status appears anywhere on this site. Every buy button sends the reader to Amazon, where that data is served live.

Source: SRC-02

Q-05Answered

Is the disclosure in the right place, and does it say enough?

What we have
A disclosure strip sits under the masthead on every page, above the content, and is repeated in the footer. It states the Associates relationship in the sentence the Operating Agreement requires.
What is missing
Nothing structural. The FTC frames placement as a judgement about prominence, and says the closer the disclosure is to the recommendation, the better (SRC-03).

Answer

Above the fold on every page, repeated in the footer, and reinforced with a 'Paid links' label next to it. The FTC guidance explicitly treats a bare 'affiliate link' label as possibly insufficient, which is why the strip carries a full sentence as well as the label.

Source: SRC-01, SRC-03

Q-06Answered

Two of the five products are thermometers. Do they belong on this site?

What we have
P-04 and P-05 resolve to meat thermometers. Three products are air fryers.
What is missing
Nothing factual. The question is editorial positioning, and a decision was taken.

Yes, in their own section. A probe is a measuring tool that moves between appliances, and it is the cheapest way to make an air fryer predictable. Filing it under Air Fryers would have been inaccurate.

Source: Internal decision, recorded in _src/data/products.json meta.categories

Q-16Answered

Q-18Answered

What may a page say about a product beyond its listing title?

What we have
Editorial rule: a stated feature may be explained in terms of what it changes for the cook, and nothing may be described as tested, measured or compared in use.
What is missing
A written house style for the owner to approve or amend.

Answer

Explain the trade, not the performance. 'Two independent drawers mean two temperatures at once' is a reading of a stated feature. 'Cooked evenly' would be a test claim and is banned by the checker.

Source: _tools/check.py; about/index.html#how-we-link

Q-21Answered

Can the site show product photographs?

What we have
Editorial illustrations generated for this publication, each labelled as an illustration and none presented as the product.
What is missing
A permitted source of product imagery. Amazon product images come with licence conditions, and the Associates programme has its own rules about Program Content.

Answer

No product photography is used. Every image is an illustration and says so on the page and in the image policy.

Source: review/images.html
