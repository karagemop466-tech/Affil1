Why this page exists

A suggestion queue that does not name its own preconditions is a wish list. Each proposal on the suggestions page points back to one of the rows below, so a reviewer can see what has to be true before a proposal is worth approving.

The rule is the same one the rest of the site runs on. An answer counts when an outside reader can open the source and check it. Where no source exists yet, the row says what would create one — usually a screenshot, a sentence from the operator, or a single test click.

Read the gates in order

Gate one asks whether a click can be credited to this site at all. Gate two asks what the site is allowed to show. Gate three asks whether a publication exists behind it. Gate four asks whether anything is being measured. Doing gate three work while gate one is open is how affiliate sites end up looking finished and earning nothing.

D-01 Open

Is a click from this site credited to shopnow047c-20?

Why this is on the list
Program Policies 2(b) requires the tag to appear as a parameter in the URL of each link. The site now builds every buy link that way. Whether Amazon records the click is a separate question and can only be answered by the click report.
What we already have
  • The tag must be a parameter in each link URL, not merely present somewhere in the session. (SRC-02)
  • Amazon names untagged product links as a reason applications are rejected. (SRC-07)
  • All five supplied short links were opened in this session and none of the arrival URLs carried a tag parameter. This is an observation about the URL after redirect, not proof about the redirect service. (review/verification.html)
What is missing
One click that appears in the Associates click report for the day it happened. Also unknown: whether the supplied short links applied the tag server-side before redirecting.
How to find it out
Click one tagged link from this site on the account holder's own device, note the time, then open Reports > Clicks in Associates Central for that date.
The artefact that closes it
A dated screenshot or CSV export of the click report showing a non-zero click count.
Who can supply it
Account holder (requires sign-in)
Cost
About ten minutes, plus however long the report takes to update.
What it unblocks
Every revenue statement, and suggestion S-01.
D-02 Open

Which product did the operator intend each link to reach?

Why this is on the list
All five links now resolve to a live listing, and the arrival ASINs are recorded. What cannot be read from a nine-character short-link code is which product was meant. Until that is confirmed, the site can describe what arrived but not claim it is what was intended.
What we already have
  • A supplied code is nine characters and contains lowercase letters; an ASIN is ten alphanumeric characters. A code is therefore not an ASIN. (SRC-06)
  • The five arrival ASINs recorded on 2026-11-20 are B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5 and B0DZFD6R9Z. (src/data/products.json)
What is missing
The intended ASIN for each of the five links, and a yes/no on whether each arrival is the product meant.
How to find it out
In Associates Central, use SiteStripe on each intended product page and copy the tagged link; or simply confirm each of the five arrivals by eye against the recorded titles.
The artefact that closes it
A list of five ASINs in the owner's own words, or five SiteStripe links.
Who can supply it
Account holder
Cost
Fifteen minutes.
What it unblocks
Any sentence of the form 'we recommend this product', and the intended/observed comparison the register currently leaves half-empty.
D-03 Open

What is the state of the Associates account, and when does the 180-day clock end?

Why this is on the list
Amazon reviews the application after three qualifying sales within the first 180 days, and states that a rejected application cannot be reassessed. A site that sends traffic before the links are right can spend the account's only attempt.
What we already have
  • Amazon requires at least three qualifying sales within the first 180 days; personal orders do not qualify. (SRC-07)
What is missing
The application date, the account's current status, and how many qualifying sales have been recorded.
How to find it out
Open the Associates dashboard home and record the status line and the application date.
The artefact that closes it
A dated screenshot of the dashboard status, with personal information cropped.
Who can supply it
Account holder
Cost
Five minutes.
What it unblocks
Whether to prioritise traffic now or to finish the content bar first; and the risk assessment for every publishing decision.
D-04 Open

Would this site clear Amazon's originality bar if it were reviewed today?

Why this is on the list
The published bar is 'robust original content (even when advertising is removed)', with a rule of thumb of at least ten posts. The site has the count. What it does not have is first-hand testing, and the guides say so in every one.
What we already have
  • Amazon states the originality bar and the ten-post rule of thumb, and says applications are rejected if they do not meet the standards. (SRC-07)
  • No product on this site has been bought, used or measured; every guide states this. (guides/index.html)
What is missing
A human judgement on whether question-shaped checklists count as original content, and whether any first-hand evidence exists anywhere.
How to find it out
Read SRC-07 against the twelve published pages and score each one: does it contain something a competitor could not have written without the same work? If not, the honest answer is no.
The artefact that closes it
A one-page audit, page by page, with a count of pages that contain first-hand evidence.
Who can supply it
Editor, not the account holder
Cost
An hour.
What it unblocks
The content plan, and whether S-04 (one tested product) is optional or mandatory.

D-06 Answered

Is the disclosure in an acceptable place?

Why this is on the list
The Operating Agreement requires the exact sentence; the regulation defines 'clear and conspicuous' in a way that can be tested against a page.
What we already have
  • The required sentence is 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.' (SRC-01)
  • 'Clear and conspicuous' means difficult to miss, and on the internet the disclosure should be unavoidable. (SRC-03)
  • The sentence appears above the masthead on every page and again in the footer, and each product call to action carries its own line. (tools/check.py)
What is missing
A legal reading of the surrounding wording, which this build cannot perform. Placement itself is settled.
How to find it out
Human review against SRC-01 and SRC-03.
The artefact that closes it
A signed note from whoever reviews it, with the date.
Who can supply it
Owner or counsel
Cost
An hour of reading.
What it unblocks
Nothing technical; it closes the highest-severity compliance question.

The answer on record

Yes, structurally. The strip sits above <main> on every page, repeats in the footer, and is enforced at build time rather than remembered.

Evidence: SRC-01, SRC-03

D-11 Open

Is the parallel edition retired, or does it stay?

Why this is on the list
Two editions were built in parallel on this repository. Both are served. Search engines dislike two copies of the same shop, and a reader who lands on the other one sees a different brand and a different set of claims.
What we already have
  • The root build is treated as canonical: it owns index.html, sitemap.xml and feed.xml, and the other edition's pages are still present and reachable. (COORDINATION.md)
  • The other edition's pages are now marked noindex and left in place so that no existing link breaks. (tools/legacy.py)
What is missing
Whether the owner wants the older pages deleted outright, redirected, or kept noindexed. Deleting is irreversible and destroys another session's work.
How to find it out
Owner decision, after opening one page from each edition.
The artefact that closes it
A sentence: keep noindexed, redirect, or delete.
Who can supply it
Owner
Cost
Nothing to keep as-is; an hour to redirect properly.
What it unblocks
S-18.
