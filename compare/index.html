How to read it

Three honest observations

  1. Sizes are not interchangeable. P-01's 6 quarts is two 3-quart drawers; P-03's 8 quarts is one basket; P-02's 26 quarts is a different shape again. Compare like with like.
  2. Thermometers have no capacity. Two of these five are probes. They are in the table so the catalogue is complete, not so they can be ranked against an oven.
  3. Nothing here says which is better. No product was tested. The notes explain what each shape changes, and the rest is your kitchen.

Why the price column is empty on a comparison page

Because a comparison is exactly where a wrong price does the most damage. Amazon permits a site to show price and availability only when Amazon serves that data into the page, or when it arrives through the official product APIs. This site is a set of static files and has neither.
