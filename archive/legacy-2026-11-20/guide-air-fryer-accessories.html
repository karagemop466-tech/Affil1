Air-fryer accessories:
worth the counter space?

Extra racks, liners and baskets promise flexibility. Whether they earn their keep depends on what you cook and where you store them.

Editorial guide · Good Measure Kitchen · 20 November 2026

Questions worth asking before you add anything

  • Which meals do you actually make, and would an accessory make them easier — or just add a step?
  • Is the accessory made for your exact model? Fit affects airflow, and airflow affects results.
  • Where will it live when it is not in use? Measure the storage spot first.
  • Is it dishwasher-safe according to the official care instructions? Never assume.
  • Does it add parts you need to wash after every use, or does it reduce cleanup?
