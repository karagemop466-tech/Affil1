Small kitchens

Will that air fryer fit a small kitchen?

Measure the space the appliance uses in motion, not just where it sits.

By Crisp & Counter Editorial · Updated 2026-11-20 · 3 min read

Tape out the footprint

Put removable tape on the counter where the appliance would go. Measure the available width, depth and height. When you have an exact model in mind, compare with its manual for heat and ventilation clearances.

Try the opening motion

The Emeril Lagasse listing calls out French doors; the Chefman TurboFry listing describes a basket. Mark where each would open. If either crosses a narrow walking route or crowds a kettle, check another spot.

Find a landing spot for hot parts

Pick a heat-safe surface nearby for the basket or tray. Check how you’d carry it there without reaching across the sink or a cooktop.
