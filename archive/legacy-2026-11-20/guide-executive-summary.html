Executive summary

What this is

It is built as a publication — with branded navigation, question-led buying guides, a product register, a suggestions queue, a pre-decision question list, and a transparent “flagged for review” record — rather than an audit or an AI-generated content farm.

Measurement

Privacy-preserving outbound-click counter and analytics infrastructure are built (five events: page_view, outbound_click, guide_open, search_query_referral, report_import). The self-hosted endpoint path is reserved; collection is not live until authorization, privacy notice, consent and retention decisions are confirmed. The campaign ledger is empty by design and will only accept records from authorized first-party reports. No conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.
