Executive summary

The short version,
for someone with four minutes

What exists, what was verified, what could not be, and what the next decision is.

Written 20 November 2026 · 1,600 words shorter than it could have been

What was asked for

An Amazon affiliate publication covering five supplied links: product research, comparison content, buying guides, click measurement and a compliant disclosure layer, published on GitHub Pages with a separate gate for paid links.

What was built

A static site with question-led buying guides, a five-row product register, a per-product record for each, a comparison table, fifteen open questions, twelve suggestions, a review queue, a source list with quoted wording, an empty campaign ledger, an inert analytics client and a build-time checker that fails on a broken link, a missing disclosure or a reintroduced price.

What was actually verified

All five supplied links reached listings on the latest check. P-04 had previously returned a checkpoint; later passes reported both thermometer listings in different orders for P-04 and P-05. This observation does not confirm the intended ASIN or attribution. Amazon's Associates Operating Agreement section 5, its Program Policies section 2(b), the FTC's endorsement FAQ and Google's outbound-link documentation were each read directly, and the relevant wording is quoted on the sources page.

The one finding that changes the plan

The five supplied link codes are nine characters and contain lowercase letters. Every ASIN that loaded is ten characters and uppercase. The codes are short-link slugs, not product identifiers — which means the earlier version of this repository recorded the wrong thing in its "intended ASIN" column, and that the intended product for each link cannot be read from the link at all. It has to come from the Associates dashboard.

This does not invalidate the links. It does mean nothing downstream can be confirmed without one look inside the account.

What could not be verified, and why it matters

Tracking attribution
No arrival page showed a tag parameter. Whether clicks are credited to shopnow047c-20 is unknown. This is the difference between a site that earns and a site that does not.
Price, rating, availability
Not shown anywhere, because Amazon permits them only from Amazon-served data or its API. This is deliberate and is the site's largest visible gap to a reader.
Author
None. No name would be true, so none is used. It caps how much authority the guides can claim.
Product quality
Nothing was tested. No product is ranked, scored or recommended.
Traffic and revenue
No analytics are running, no Search Console property is verified, and no commission rate has been read. Therefore no conversion score and no revenue forecast appears anywhere on this site.

The next decision

Three things, in this order. Tag the links from SiteStripe and make one test click, then read the Associates report for that day. Verify the Search Console property and submit the sitemap. Then choose between the two paths that actually improve the site: buy one product and test it properly, or wait for query data before writing another guide.

Writing more guides first is the option that looks like progress and is not.
