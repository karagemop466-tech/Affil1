Flagged for review

Every irregularity, unresolved item and known gap behind this site, with the evidence attached. None of these blocked publication — the shop is live and shoppable — but each one is a real thing to look at, and pretending otherwise would be the actual problem.

How to triage this list. FLG-002 and FLG-003 are the two that need a person, not an engineering session: one is a question of intent, the other a question of policy. FLG-001 needs a corrected link. The rest are engineering or account work that can be scheduled. Nothing here is a fabricated finding invented to look thorough — if it is listed, it was encountered.
FLG-001 · Open — unresolved

One submitted link does not resolve to a product

What was found
The link https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59 returned an Amazon bot-check interstitial on first fetch, then the Amazon homepage on retry. A direct request to the ASIN page for B0C8MOW59 returned no product. No listing was ever reached.
Why it matters
One of five submitted products is absent from the shop. No page has been created for it and it appears nowhere on the site except as a held link.
Evidence
Repeat fetches on 2026-11-20. Two outcomes only: /checkpoint/continue interstitial, and amazon.com homepage.
Suggested action
Regenerate the link in Associates Central or send the product name. The page will be built on receipt.

FLG-002 · Open — needs owner input

No intended ASIN has been confirmed for any link

What was found
All four resolvable links were followed and their destinations recorded. What was never supplied is the ASIN each link was meant to point at. The record therefore shows four observed targets and zero confirmed intentions.
Why it matters
Product pages describe the listing that the link actually reached. If a link was meant to point at a different variant — and two of these listings offer multiple sizes or probe counts — a published page could be promoting a variant nobody chose.
Evidence
See the product intent record; every row is marked observed only.
Suggested action
Confirm the intended ASIN and, where the listing offers variants, the intended variant.

FLG-003 · Open — not a finding either way

Compliance status of the link.amazon short-link format is unresolved

What was found
The submitted links use the link.amazon shortener and carry no visible Associates tag in the supplied form. The Operating Agreement requires Special Links in the tagged format and forbids shortening that makes it unclear a link goes to Amazon. Whether this specific format, generated inside Associates Central, satisfies that is not established by anything read in this pass.
Why it matters
This record deliberately makes no claim that the links are invalid. It also cannot claim they are valid. Published outbound links therefore use the canonical /dp/ url with the supplied tag instead of the short form, which is compliant on its face regardless of how the short-link question resolves.
Evidence
Program Policies, Participation Requirements, especially the Special Links section and the shortening clause. The supplied codes are also not 10-character ASINs, which is an observation about shape and not a compliance conclusion.
Suggested action
Regenerate one link in Associates Central's link builder and compare the url shape; optionally open a support case with Amazon Associates.

FLG-004 · Open — needs a host

The click counter is shipped but cannot record anything

What was found
The measurement beacon, the endpoint contract and the five event definitions are all in place. CRISP_ENDPOINT is null, so the beacon returns before making any request.
Why it matters
Every click figure on the site is zero because nothing is being measured, not because nothing happened. No conversion or traffic claim can be made from this site's own data.
Evidence
assets/js/analytics.js, and the measurement record.
Suggested action
Choose a host, publish a privacy notice, set a retention period, then set the endpoint constant.

FLG-008 · Known gap

No outbound link-health check

What was found
The build gate checks internal links only. It cannot fetch amazon.com at build time, so a delisted product or a changed ASIN would not be caught automatically.
Why it matters
Amazon requires removal of program content that is no longer displayed. A dead product page is a compliance defect, not merely a broken link.
Evidence
scripts/check.mjs scope.
Suggested action
Add a scheduled workflow that fetches each outbound url weekly and opens an issue on failure.

FLG-009 · Open — observation variance

Short-link resolution is not stable across fetches

What was found
Three fetch passes over the same five short links on the same day produced different destinations. Two passes (obs-1, obs-2 in data/product-intent.json) resolved B00hJ57DV to /dp/B08DKYBTPH and B0f3GKalj to /dp/B0DZFD6R9Z; an earlier pass (obs-0) saw both land on the Amazon homepage. B0c8moW59 has only ever returned the continue-shopping interstitial. Amazon appears to gate short-link redirects behind bot checks, so any single observation is point-in-time evidence, not a guarantee.
Why it matters
Observed redirect targets must stay observations (never promoted to intended ASINs), and a destination that resolved once may not resolve for every reader. The shop's published outbound links therefore use canonical /dp/ URLs with the tag, not the short links.
Evidence
data/product-intent.json observation_log: obs-0 vs obs-1 and obs-2, all 2026-11-20, all against Amazon.com official site.
Suggested action
Re-run the observation pass from the enrolled Associates workflow (one link per product, destination plus tracking report confirmed) before scaling. Do not assert the short links are invalid — see FLG-003.

FLG-010 · Resolved — records preserved

Legacy data registers were contradictory and have been reconciled, not deleted

What was found
Parallel sessions left three product registers that disagreed: data/product-intent.json (observed-only, fields separate), data/confirmations.json (called two rows 'verified' and merged observation into the listing-url field), and data/products.json (called everything unverified and claimed the build environment could not reach Amazon). The registers also used different status vocabularies.
Why it matters
A reader of the raw data could have drawn three different conclusions about the same five links. The published pages render only from the canonical register.
Evidence
data/confirmations.json rows conf-001..005; data/products.json meta.note; data/product-intent.json field_rules.
Suggested action
None required. data/product-intent.json is canonical; the other two carry supersession notes and are kept as history. Owners should treat 'observed_only' as the standing status until intent is confirmed in Associates Central.
