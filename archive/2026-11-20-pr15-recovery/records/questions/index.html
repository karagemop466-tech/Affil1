What we do not yet know

Fourteen questions that stand between this site and a confident decision. For each: what we actually hold, what is missing, what the gap blocks, who can close it, and the smallest step that would close it. Nothing here has been filled in by inference.

How to use this page. Each row is a blocker, not a to-do. If a question here is still open, the decision it blocks should not be made — that is the whole point of listing them. The three that block the most are Q-01 (which ASIN was intended), Q-02 (whether the submitted link format is compliant) and Q-06 (where the counter will run).

Q-01Which ASIN did each of the five short links intend to point at?

We hold
Observed redirect targets for four of five links (P1, P2, P3, P5). A redirect is evidence of behaviour, not a statement of intent.
Missing
A statement from the owner, or an Associates tracking report, naming the intended ASIN for each link.
Why it matters
Amazon identifies products by ASIN (S22). Without intent, we cannot tell a correct link from a link that redirects to the wrong variant.
Blocks
Publishing final product pages, and any claim that a link is correct.
Who can answer
Link owner
How to get it
Owner confirms each intended ASIN, or exports a dated click report from Associates Central showing the landing ASIN per link.
Handled today
Four products published on observed ASINs, marked observed_only. One link unresolved.

Q-02What is the compliance status of the link.amazon short-link format?

We hold
The Program Policies require Special Links with the Associates tag, and prohibit shortening that obscures an Amazon destination (S02, S03). The supplied links use link.amazon and carry no visible tag in the submitted form.
Missing
Amazon's own statement on whether link.amazon links generated inside Associates Central count as Special Links, and how the tag is carried.
Why it matters
If the format is non-compliant, publishing it puts the account at risk. If it is compliant, publishing it is fine. Both readings are available and neither is established.
Blocks
Whether to keep short links anywhere on the site.
Who can answer
Amazon Associates support, or the owner's own Associates Central link generator
How to get it
Regenerate one link in Associates Central's link builder and compare the resulting URL shape; optionally open a support case.
Handled today
Treated as unresolved (FLG-003). No claim made either way. Outbound anchors use the canonical /dp/ URL with the supplied tag.

Q-03Is shopnow047c-20 an approved, active Store ID?

We hold
The tag string as supplied by the owner. It contains no Amazon mark, which satisfies S13.
Missing
Confirmation that the ID is enrolled and in good standing. Associates Central requires sign-in (S17).
Why it matters
An unapproved tag earns nothing, and the site would be advertising a track that does not exist.
Blocks
Any revenue expectation, and every claim on the measurement page.
Who can answer
Associates account holder
How to get it
Sign in to Associates Central and read the Store ID and account status.
Handled today
Tag is used as supplied. No revenue is claimed anywhere.

Q-05Is there real search demand for the four guide topics?

We hold
Four working titles, chosen as editorial hypotheses. No volume, difficulty, or interest data.
Missing
Search Console query data, or any keyword tool export. Search Console is not connected.
Why it matters
The brief says pick the next topic from measured queries, not instinct. Without measurement the cadence is guesswork.
Blocks
Guide five, and the decision to scale content volume.

Owner (property verification), then data
How to get it
Verify the Pages URL in Search Console, submit sitemap.xml, wait for impressions.
Handled today

Q-06Where will the click endpoint actually run?

We hold
A defined, documented endpoint contract and a client beacon (SG-05). No server.
Missing
A host for the write-only endpoint, and the retention period for the counters.
Why it matters
Until a host exists, the beacon fires into nothing and every click count is zero.
Blocks
Reporting any click number as real.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it
Pick a host (a small function endpoint is enough) and set its retention window.
Handled today
Beacon is inert and says so. The measurement page shows zero and labels it as not connected.

Q-07Who is the author of record?

We hold
An organisation byline: 'the shopnow047c-20 desk'.
Missing
A real person's name, and their basis for writing about kitchen appliances.
Why it matters
Authorship is the difference between a publication and a generated site, and the FTC rules on untested product claims (S19) make an author's actual experience the limiting factor on what can be said.
Blocks
First-person testing claims, and the author page.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it
Supply a name and a one-line credential.
Handled today
No first-person claims anywhere. Copy is written as comparison criteria, not as verdicts.

Q-08What is the canonical base URL, and is it the only host in play?

We hold
GitHub Pages is enabled and built, publishing from main at the repo's Pages URL (S21).
Missing
Confirmation that no custom domain or second host will also serve the site.
Why it matters
Two hosts serving the same pages split ranking signals and create duplicate canonicals (S20).
Blocks
Canonical, sitemap, and feed absolute URLs.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it
Confirm whether a custom domain is planned. If yes, change one constant in the data file and rebuild.
Handled today
One base URL constant, self-referencing canonicals, one sitemap, one feed.

Q-09What privacy notice and consent basis apply to the click counter?

We hold
A counter designed to store no identifier: no cookie, no IP, no user agent, no session id. Only an aggregate per-product, per-day count.
Missing
A written privacy notice, and a decision on whether the counter is even personal data under the applicable regime.
Why it matters
The Participation Requirements put the data-disclosure duty on the associate (S24). Aggregate counters are low risk; an unwritten notice is still a gap.
Blocks
Turning the counter on in production.
Who can answer
Owner, with legal review if the site serves EU or UK readers
How to get it
Publish a short privacy notice describing the counter, then enable the endpoint.
Handled today
Counter shipped inert. No third-party script, so no third-party cookie.

Five links. Four resolved to three air fryers and one meat thermometer.
Missing

Blocks
A 'why we picked these' section, and any ranking.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it
State the criterion in one sentence, or send a wider list to choose from.
Handled today
No ranking, no 'best of' claim. Pages describe decision criteria instead.

Q-12What does 'conversion' mean here, and who reports it?

Blocks
Any conversion rate shown on the site.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it

Handled today

Q-13Do the products have variant ambiguity that a single ASIN hides?

We hold
Two of the four resolved listings offer multiple sizes or probe counts at their own URLs.
Missing
Which variant the owner intends for each.
Why it matters
An ASIN can be variant-specific. The April 2026 change narrows onsite commission to the same ASIN variant as the linked detail page (S15 context). Linking a 3-probe page when the intent was 2 probes earns on the wrong item.
Blocks
Final confirmation of P2 and P5.
Who can answer
Owner
How to get it
State the intended variant alongside the ASIN.
Handled today
Published pages describe the family and the observed listing; no variant claim is made.

Q-14What happens if a listing is delisted or the ASIN changes?

We hold
A build gate that checks internal links, and a dated observation per product.
Missing
An outbound link-health check, which cannot run in the build without network access at build time.
Why it matters
Amazon requires removal of Program Content that is no longer displayed (S02, content clause). A dead product page is a compliance defect, not just a bad link.
Blocks
Nothing today. Becomes blocking once traffic is meaningful.
Who can answer
Engineering
How to get it
Add a scheduled workflow that fetches each outbound URL weekly and opens an issue on failure.
Handled today
Recorded as a known gap on the Flagged for Review page.

What would let all of this be decided at once

Three short inputs would close five of the fourteen questions: the intended ASIN for each link, a named author, and a decision on whether the short-link format will be kept. That is a small amount of information carrying a large amount of unblocking, which is why it is listed here rather than asked quietly by email.
