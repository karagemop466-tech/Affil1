How to decide on this one

Sync finish, and why it is the whole point

Dual-basket machines are not two air fryers in a trench coat; the useful feature is that the two baskets can be told to land at the same moment. Without sync, you are timing two cooks by hand. Check that the model you are looking at has it, because some dual-basket units do not.

Independent 3-quart zones versus one 6-quart basket

Six quarts in one basket is better for a single large item. Six quarts split in two is better for two foods that need different temperatures or different times. These are different appliances that happen to share a number on the box, and the number is the least useful way to compare them.

The window matters more than it sounds

Every time you open a basket to check, you drop the temperature and add time. A viewing window is a small feature that quietly changes how often you get the timing right.
