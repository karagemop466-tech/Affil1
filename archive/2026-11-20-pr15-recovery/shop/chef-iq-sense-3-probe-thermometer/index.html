How to decide on this one

Why a thermometer belongs next to an air fryer

Air fryers cook fast on the outside and can lag in the middle, especially with bone-in poultry. A probe in the thickest part turns a guess into a reading, which is the difference between juicy and safe-but-dry.

Ambient sensing changes what you can infer

An ambient sensor reads the cooking environment alongside the food. It lets the app estimate cooking time rather than just reporting a current temperature, which is the feature that separates a smart thermometer from a display on a wire.

Probe count is the variant question

One, two, three and four-probe versions appear on the same listing. The count changes the price and what you can cook at once, so pick the variant deliberately rather than accepting whichever the link opens.
