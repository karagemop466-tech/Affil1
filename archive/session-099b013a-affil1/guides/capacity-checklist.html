A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Working title — not a claim about popularity, search volume or product superiority.

“Quart” is the most misused number in air fryer shopping. Use this checklist instead of the box front:

CheckWhy it matters
Usable basket area, not just quartsTwo 6-quart models can hold very different amounts depending on basket shape.
Layer cooking / rack depthStacking food blocks airflow; deeper single layers often cook more evenly.
Basket countDual baskets let you cook two foods at once — check whether zones sync finish times.
Household portion fitMatch the basket to your typical meal (one chicken breast vs a family tray), not to a maximum.
Counter footprint vs capacityBigger capacity usually means a bigger, heavier machine that stays put.
