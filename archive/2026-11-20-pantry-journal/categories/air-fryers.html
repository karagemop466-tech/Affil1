Air fryers

Air fryers, by shape

Three machines, two shapes: a drawer you shake and a door you open. The shape decides how you cook more than the badge does.

An air fryer is a small, fast convection oven. The useful distinction is not brand or wattage — it is whether the food comes out in a basket you can shake or on a tray you slide into a cavity. Every other trade-off follows from that.

CHEFMAN

Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer

Two independent drawers, each the size of a small basket fryer. The listing is explicit about the split: 6 quarts total means 2 x 3 quarts, not one deep 6-quart basket.
