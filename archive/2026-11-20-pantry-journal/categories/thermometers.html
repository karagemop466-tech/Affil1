Thermometers

Thermometers, by job

One checks the temperature now. One tells you when it will be ready. They answer different questions, and the cheap one is not the lesser one.

An instant-read answers a safety question: is the middle of this chicken at 165°F. A wireless leave-in answers a timing question: when will it be. Most kitchens only need the first, and the second is a convenience rather than a requirement.
