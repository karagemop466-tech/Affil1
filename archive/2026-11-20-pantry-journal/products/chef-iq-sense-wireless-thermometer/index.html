Thermometers · Wireless leave-in thermometer

CHEF iQ Sense Wireless Meat Thermometer

A leave-in probe that talks to a phone. It answers a different question from a handheld: not what is the temperature now, but when will it be ready.

The quick take

This is for the cook who puts something in the oven and wants to leave the room. It is not a replacement for an instant-read, because you still need a second opinion when you are checking several pieces. 'Unlimited Range' is the seller's phrase; what it really depends on is your WiFi.

Who this shape suits

  • You roast, smoke or grill and want an alert before the food is overdone.
  • You cook more than one piece at a time and want a probe in each.
  • You are comfortable with a phone app in the kitchen.

Check these before you buy

  • Ask what happens without WiFi. If the app is the only display, a dropped connection is a blind cook.
  • Check the maximum probe temperature against how you cook, and read '1000°F heat safe' as the probe's limit, not the oven's.
  • Confirm the app's platform requirements and whether it needs an account.
