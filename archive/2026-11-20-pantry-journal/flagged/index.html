What is unverified, and what it would take to settle it

Nothing on this page blocks the site from being live. That is the point of publishing it: a shop that hides its gaps has gaps you cannot see. Each item states what was seen, what could not be confirmed, and the one action that would close it.

high The submitted codes are not ASINs FLG-101

All five submitted codes are nine characters. Amazon defines an ASIN as a unique ten-character alphanumeric code. The codes are short-link slugs, not product identifiers. The shop records the submitted slug and the observed ASIN in separate fields, and never substitutes one for the other.

What you would do: If you intended these slugs to be ASINs, they are not. Nothing needs changing on the site unless the intended products differ from the five observed.

Check it: https://sell.amazon.com/blog/what-is-an-asin

high Tracking attribution to shopnow047c-20 is unconfirmed FLG-102

No observed destination URL visibly carried the tag parameter. Amazon requires the Associates ID as a URL parameter on each link. Whether the short-link redirector attaches the tag server-side on a real click cannot be seen from a fetch.

What you would do: One click, signed in, and check the tracking report. Record the date and the result.

Check it: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

high Compliance status of the link.amazon short links remains unresolved FLG-103

The links redirect through a shortener. Amazon's policies prohibit a shortened link that obscures the Amazon destination, and require the tag to be a parameter on the link. The shop does not claim the supplied links are invalid, and does not use them for its buy buttons. It links directly to tagged amazon.com/dp/ URLs instead, and lists the supplied link on each product page for transparency.

What you would do: Run the one-link test. If the redirector preserves the tag and lands visibly on Amazon, the supplied links can be reconsidered.

Check it: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement

medium No commission rate is known or used FLG-104

The commission schedule sits behind the Associates sign-in. No rate is published, and no revenue, earnings estimate or forecast appears anywhere on the site.

What you would do: Read the rate card while signed in and record it with a date if it is ever needed for a calculation.

Check it: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

medium No price, rating, review count or availability is published anywhere FLG-105

Program Policies 2(b) permits these only where Amazon serves the link carrying the data, or where it arrives through the Creators API or PA API. Neither condition holds, so the absence is policy-driven and permanent until the API is connected.

What you would do: Apply for Creators API access if live prices are wanted. See SUG-22.

Check it: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

medium Same-day observations disagree for three of five links FLG-106

Three links were recorded by an earlier session as returning the Amazon homepage or a bot checkpoint, and returned product pages in this pass. Amazon varies its response by client and time. A single observation does not establish a stable destination, and the site does not present one as settled.

What you would do: Repeat the observation over several days, or confirm destinations inside the Associates workflow instead of by fetch.

No external source: this is an observation about this repository.

medium The appliances have not been tested FLG-107

Every capability statement on this site is the seller's own listing language, labelled as such. No measurement, no test and no hands-on comparison was performed. The FTC's guidance requires an endorsement to reflect the endorser's honest experience, which is why nothing here is framed as a verdict.

What you would do: Buy and test one unit at a time. See SUG-23.

Check it: https://consumer.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

low No analytics, no cookies, and therefore no traffic numbers FLG-108

The site ships a small event script that transmits nothing unless an endpoint is configured, and none is. GitHub Pages cannot run a collector. Any statement about how many people visit or click would be invented, so none is made.

What you would do: Decide on a collector or a hosted analytics service. See SUG-24.

Check it: https://docs.github.com/en/pages

low Product imagery is illustration, not the product FLG-109

The food photographs on this site were generated for it and show no appliance. Amazon's IP licence restricts use of Amazon-served imagery, and presenting a stock photo as the product would misrepresent it. Each image carries a caption saying what it is.

What you would do: Replace with genuine photographs once the appliances are tested, if desired.

Check it: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

low Two older sections of this repository still publish overlapping pages FLG-110

The repository root and crisp/ contain earlier publications covering the same topics. This session did not edit them because they belong to other sessions' scopes. They are listed on the Site map page so they can be retired deliberately.

What you would do: Decide between redirect, archive or leave. See SUG-16.

No external source: this is an observation about this repository.
