Before any decision

The questions that come before a choice

Sixteen questions, split by what it would take to answer them. A decision made without the ones in the lower two groups is a decision made on a guess, and the shop would rather show you where the guess is.

Nothing on this page is a recommendation. The first group is settled and sourced; the second is work this project can do; the third needs you in the Associates dashboard; the fourth cannot be answered with the access this project has at all.

Q-01
Answered		What exact wording does Amazon require an associate to display?“As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” The Agreement requires it be stated clearly and prominently on the Site.Operating Agreement, section 5 (Updated October 15, 2025)
Q-02
Answered		May this site show prices, stock or star ratings?Only where Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or where it arrives through the Creators API or PA API. No such feed is connected, so nothing of the kind is published anywhere on this site.Program Policies, section 2(b) (Updated April 14, 2026)
Q-03
Answered		What is an ASIN, and is the code in a supplied short link one?An ASIN is “a unique 10-character alphanumeric code assigned to every product listed in the Amazon store”, and it appears after /dp/ in a product URL. All five supplied codes are 9 characters, so they are short-link slugs, not ASINs. The shop uses the 10-character /dp/ code observed on the destination page and keeps the supplied slug in a separate field.Amazon Seller Central blog, Understanding ASINs (Jun 4, 2025)
Q-04
Answered		How must affiliate links be marked for search engines?Google asks that advertisements or paid placements be marked with rel="sponsored"; multiple rel values may be combined. Every outbound Amazon link on this site carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".Google Search Central, Qualify your outbound links (last updated 2025-12-10)
Q-05
Answered		Where must the affiliate disclosure appear, and how prominently?The FTC asks for disclosures that are clear and conspicuous, and says a connection between an endorser and a marketer should be disclosed where it will be seen. On this site the statement sits in a strip directly under the masthead on every page and is repeated in the footer, so it precedes the first buy link.FTC Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking
Q-06
Answered		What are the official safe minimum internal cooking temperatures?Poultry 165°F; ground meats 160°F; whole cuts of beef, pork, veal and lamb 145°F with a three-minute rest; fish and shellfish 145°F; leftovers and casseroles 165°F.USDA FSIS, Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (updated Apr 14, 2025)
