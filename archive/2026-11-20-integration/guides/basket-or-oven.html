Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Working title — not a claim about popularity, search volume or product superiority.

Air fryers generally land in two shapes. This guide is a comparison frame, not a verdict:

  • Basket-style — a pull-out basket, usually compact, built around one cooking zone. Easier to shake food mid-cycle; typically smaller footprint.
  • Oven-style (French door / multi-function) — a front-opening door with racks, often larger capacity and extra modes (bake, broil, dehydrate, rotisserie). Takes more counter space.

What to compare before choosing

  • Capacity. Quart ratings are not standardized across brands; check usable basket area, not just the number.
  • Counter space. Oven-style models need door clearance; measure the strip of counter where it will live.
  • Cleanup. Dishwasher-safe baskets and crisper trays versus fixed racks and drip trays.
  • Controls. Digital presets versus manual dials — more presets do not mean better results.
