Questions that need answers before decisions

Fourteen questions, each with the information required to answer it and a plain statement of whether this session could. 3 are answered. 9 are blocked, and none of them is blocked for want of effort — they are blocked because the answer needs an account, a decision or a person.

The rule behind this table. A blocked question is not an invitation to guess. Where the answer is unknown, the value on the site is blank and the row below says why. That is why a product card shows no price and a guide names no winner.

Unknown. blocked on account Open each link while signed in to the Associates dashboard and check that the click registers under shopnow047c-20. FLG-002

Do not synthesise a verdict from listing titles. FLG-007
Q-07 Would any of these products appeal to readers enough to click? Outbound click data from a first-party endpoint, over a meaningful window. No data exists yet. The ledger is intentionally empty. blocked on decision Choose a first-party host, deploy the documented collector, then wait for enough sessions to mean anything. FLG-009
Q-08 Which guide topic should be written next? Search Console query impressions, and the click-through rate of existing guides. Undetermined. Four guides are published as hypotheses; none has been measured. blocked on account Verify the site in Search Console, submit the sitemap, and revisit after four to six weeks. FLG-011
Q-09 Under what name should the site publish, and who is the author? A real legal or pen name, a short bio, and a decision on whether to disclose testing or not. Undetermined. No author identity is published, because inventing one would be a fabrication. blocked on decision Supply the name and a two-sentence bio. Then an author page and a real byline can be published. FLG-013
Q-10 Is the site allowed to show Amazon product photographs? Either an Amazon-served widget that carries the image, or API image fields used unmodified under the IP License. Not established for this site. Program Content may not be altered, and this site has no licensed feed. blocked on account Enrol the API, or use Amazon-served link formats, and then place images unmodified. FLG-015
Q-11 Does the FTC require the disclosure to be a specific wording? The FTC's own guidance on affiliate disclosure wording and placement. Answered. The FTC gives example wording and says the disclosure must be clear and conspicuous, and that the reader must be able to weigh the recommendation. Nearby 'paid link' labelling is described as adequate. answered n/a —
Q-12 May this site display price comparison tables? The Associates Program rules on price display, including the comparison-format clause. Answered as a constraint. A price may be shown only if Amazon serves it or it arrives via the API, and a comparison format must show both the lowest new and, if provided, lowest used price. This site shows neither. answered n/a FLG-006

Missing information, grouped by who can supply it

Only the account owner can supply

  • Whether each link carries the tracking ID
  • Whether the short links are compliant Special Links
  • The intended ASIN for each product
  • The commission rate for each category
  • Search Console access for the domain
  • API credentials, if live prices are wanted

Only a decision can supply

  • The publishing name and author identity
  • Whether the site will ever test products
  • Whether a third-party analytics provider is acceptable
  • Whether the site stays on the Pages URL or gets a domain
  • Whether email capture is worth the compliance surface
