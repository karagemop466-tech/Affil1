What shipped

What stays behind the gate

Marketing links, account data, prices, reviews and commission figures are not published here. Review the Associates Operating Agreement, Program Policies, FTC affiliate guidance and Google’s outbound link guidance before publishing. The GitHub Pages usage limits rule out using Pages for a site primarily directed at commercial transactions. Reader capture is deferred; use RSS without asking for an email address.

Proposed publishing cadence

Every other Thursday: review available query data and propose a single buying question. Publish or revise a sourced guide only after evidence and compliance review. Update canonical URLs, scope sitemap and feed in the same build. If there are no measured queries yet, label topics as editorial hypotheses instead of inventing demand. Check crawl coverage in Search Console; a sitemap helps discovery but is not proof of indexing.

Measurement and privacy

No measurement is enabled on this Pages edition. A self-hosted companion can count five events without cookies or stored visitor identifiers after the hosting and privacy review. It does not record purchases or collect email addresses.
