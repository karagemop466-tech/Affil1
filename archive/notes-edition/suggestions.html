DECISION DESK · SUGGESTIONS QUEUE

Proposals under review —
choose before you build.

Every proposed change to this site lands here first, with the reasoning, the effort, the dependencies and the gate that must pass before implementation. Nothing on this page has been implemented on its own recommendation alone; a reviewer picks the path and records the decision.

How to use this queue

  • Statuses: Awaiting decision = ready to review · Adopted = approved and scheduled · Blocked = a gate must pass first (e.g. account access, policy review).
  • Decision gate: the specific evidence or permission that must exist before work starts. No gate, no build.
  • Pick a path, write it down: mark one item Adopted at a time, record who decided and when, then implement. This page is the paper trail.

Suggestions for improving decision-making itself

01

Gate, then build

Every suggestion above carries a decision gate. Adopt the rule: no implementation starts until its gate evidence exists — this is what keeps enthusiasm from outrunning verification.

02

One decision owner

Name a single reviewer for this queue (even if it's you). Split ownership produces unqueued changes; unqueued changes are how sites drift into slop.

03

Write the decision down

A decision that isn't recorded didn't happen. Mark the row Adopted, date it, and note the evidence used — the queue becomes the site's decision history.

04

Measure before optimizing

Resist auto-optimization until the ledger has first-party rows. The order is fixed: verify → publish → measure → then optimize, with the ledger as the referee.

Nothing here is silently shipped
