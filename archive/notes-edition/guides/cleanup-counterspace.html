EVERYDAY USE · BUYER'S NOTEBOOK

Cleanup, counter space
and the everyday test.

The best appliance on paper is the one you still enjoy washing in March. Walk the whole routine — out, cook, table, wash, store — and score it for your kitchen, not a showroom.

Editorial guide · Good Measure Kitchen · 20 November 2026 · 4 min read

Walk the whole routine once

Imagine one typical dinner from end to end: pull the appliance out (or is it already out?), prep, cook, move food to the table, wash the parts, dry them, put everything away. Note each step where the routine could become friction — a heavy lift from a low cabinet, a drawer that needs two hands, a basket that doesn't fit the sink. Friction you can imagine today is friction you'll feel every day.

The counter residency question

Appliances earn counter residency one of two ways: they're light enough to move daily, or useful enough to stay out. Neither is wrong — but they make different demands. If it stays out, measure the footprint plus door or drawer clearance plus heat-safe spacing from the official manual. If it moves, weigh it — the official listing lists shipping weight at least; treat it as a floor, not the full picture, and leave unconfirmed values blank.

Inventory the food-contact parts

  • Take the official manual for the exact model and list every removable part: basket, tray, rack, crumb tray, liner, drip pan.
  • Check the care instructions per part — never assume "dishwasher-safe" across the set.
  • Picture each part in your sink and your dish rack. Wide drawers and tall racks are the usual geometry surprises.
  • Ask what happens between meals: crumb trays and drip pans either earn their keep or become the part you skip cleaning.

The storage plan

Decide before buying where every accessory will live: shelf, cabinet, drawer, or back in the box (the box always wins the shelf war it starts). Measure that space too. An appliance with six accessories is really one appliance plus six storage decisions.
