Findings

Nine things this pass changed

Two of them changed what the site says. One of them changed whether the site can earn.

V-01

The link that failed three times resolved twice

What was observed
P-04 returned Amazon's /checkpoint/continue page on three consecutive attempts in an earlier session. In this session it resolved on both attempts to /dp/B0DHZTCNR5.
What it means
The link is not dead. Either the interstitial is intermittent, or it is served to some clients and not others. A register that recorded only one of those outcomes would be misleading in one direction or the other.
What the site did about it
The P-04 record now carries both outcomes, the product is on the site, and the retry is the reason a monthly link check is on the suggestion list.

Check it yourself

  • https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DHZTCNR5
V-02

None of the five arrival URLs carried a tracking parameter

What was observed
After following redirects, all five links ended on a bare /dp/<ASIN> address. No tag parameter appeared in any of them.
What it means
Program Policies 2(b) requires the Associates ID as a parameter in the URL of each link, and Amazon's own help page lists untagged product links as a reason applications are rejected. Whether the redirect service applies the tag server-side could not be observed from outside, so the short links were neither trusted nor declared invalid.
What the site did about it
The site stopped offering the short links as click targets. Every buy link is now built as /dp/<ASIN>/?tag=shopnow047c-20 from the ASIN observed on arrival, the submitted code is kept in the record, and a build check fails if an untagged Amazon link appears. Attribution is still unconfirmed until a click shows in the report.

Check it yourself

  • https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
V-03

Two of the five products are thermometers, not one

What was observed
P-04 resolved to a ThermoMaven instant-read probe. P-05 resolves to a CHEF iQ wireless probe set. Amazon's breadcrumb files both under Kitchen Utensils & Gadgets, then Thermometers & Timers, then Meat.
What it means
The catalogue is three air fryers and two thermometers. Every page that described the site as four air fryers and a thermometer was wrong, and a site that called P-04 the fourth fryer would have been worse.
What the site did about it
Front page, register, comparison and guides were corrected, the product card now exists, and a thermometer guide was written around the two products.

V-04

The disclosure clause is unchanged, and the policies page carries a date

What was observed
Operating Agreement section 5 states the required sentence exactly as quoted. The Program Policies page states 'Updated: April 14, 2026' and section 2(b) contains both the tag requirement and the price/availability rule.
What it means
The two claims the site leans on hardest are current, and a reviewer can re-read both without an account.
What the site did about it
Quotes re-verified verbatim and kept on the sources page with the date read.

V-05

Amazon does publish an ASIN definition a reader can open

What was observed
Sell on Amazon states that an ASIN is a unique 10-character alphanumeric code and that it appears after /dp/ in the URL.
What it means
The earlier flag saying this definition could not be reached was too pessimistic: Seller Central help is behind a sign-in, but the seller blog is not.
What the site did about it
FLG-011 is closed with this source, and the nine-character code versus ten-character ASIN argument now rests on an Amazon page rather than on an observation alone.

  • https://sell.amazon.com/blog/what-is-an-asin

V-07

Google's paid-link guidance is unchanged

What was observed
The outbound-links documentation, last updated 2025-12-10, still says to mark advertisement or paid-placement links with the sponsored value.
What it means
rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" on every Amazon link remains the documented marking, and a policy citation is still not a paid link and must not carry it.
What the site did about it
The build check still fails on an unmarked affiliate link and on a policy citation wrongly marked sponsored.

Check it yourself

  • https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links
V-08

Amazon ties the tag to account approval, and sets a content bar

What was observed
The application review page requires at least three qualifying sales in the first 180 days, asks for robust original content with ten posts as a rule of thumb, says applications are rejected if they do not meet the standard, and lists untagged product links among the reasons an application is rejected.
What it means
The missing tag was not a polish issue. It is a stated rejection reason, and the content bar is higher than 'published something'.
What the site did about it
Recorded as SRC-07, used to set the first decision gate, and written into the decision dossier as D-03, D-04 and D-17.

Check it yourself

  • https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
