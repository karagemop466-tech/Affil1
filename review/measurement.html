Measurement plan

Measurement is written,
and switched off

Five events are defined, a client exists, and nothing is being recorded. That is the current state and it is deliberate.

Beacon disabled by default · no cookies · no third-party requests · no endpoint deployed

The brief asked for a privacy-preserving outbound-click counter and a self-hosted analytics endpoint. Both exist in this repository. Neither is live, because shipping a live beacon before a privacy notice exists would be the wrong order of operations.

The client uses no cookies or browser storage. If enabled on a first-party host, it sends only allowlisted page and event names and, for clicks, the observed product ASIN. The collector keeps daily totals, not visitor records. Scroll depth does not prove reading, and clicks do not prove sessions or orders.

What has to happen before it goes live

  1. A privacy notice. What is collected, why, for how long, and who to contact. This does not exist yet.
  2. A deployed collector. The endpoint is in this repository and is not hosted anywhere. GitHub Pages serves static files only.
  3. A retention decision. Approve and enforce a retention period for daily totals and their backups.
  4. A consent decision. Whether visitor consent is required depends on jurisdiction and on what is collected. That is a legal question, not an engineering one, and it has not been answered.

Why the site currently claims nothing about traffic

Every traffic statement on this site is an absence: no sessions, no clicks, no conversions, no revenue. That is not modesty. With the beacon off, the site genuinely has no numbers, and inventing plausible ones would be the single fastest way to make everything else on the site untrustworthy.

What the campaign ledger holds

Nothing. It has a defined schema — date, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, reported orders — and every row has to arrive with a provenance note. See the empty ledger.
