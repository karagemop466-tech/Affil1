Air fryers

Three air fryers, three different shapes

A dual basket, a French-door oven and a single deep drawer. The shape decides how you cook far more than the quart figure does.

3 products · nothing tested · no prices shown

Every air fryer moves hot air over food. What changes between these three is the container the food sits in, and that change is bigger than any difference in badge or wattage.

A dual basket lets you run two jobs at once and wash four pieces afterwards. A single deep basket is the simplest machine here and the easiest to live with if your meals fit in one layer. A countertop oven with an air-fry setting is the only one of the three that accepts a tray, a pan or a plate, which is what makes it useful for baking and reheating as well as frying.

What actually decides it

Do you cook two different things at once?
Then a second basket earns its keep. If the two things need different temperatures, one basket means two sessions.
Do you want to put a dish inside?
Only the oven shape takes a tray or a plate. A deep drawer takes loose food.
How much counter and door room do you have?
Measure the space with the drawer open or the door swung. That is the footprint, not the closed dimensions.
