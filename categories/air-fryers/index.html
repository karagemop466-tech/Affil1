worth the counter space?

A basket is a deep container you fill and close. An oven is a shallow cavity with racks and a door. Capacity means nothing until you know which of those two things you are buying, which is why this page starts with shape and ends with the measurements you have to take yourself.

Before you buy

What to work out here, not on the listing

What this page cannot tell you: how any of these cooks, how loud it is, how long it lasts, or what it costs. Those are not omissions we can fix by writing harder. A listing page does not carry them in a form this site is allowed to republish, and nothing here has been measured.

How this page is built

Each product block is transcribed from the listing this project reached, and links to it. A product's identity is recorded as observed, not confirmed: the register keeps the ASIN the operator intended and the ASIN the link actually opened in separate fields, because no account owner has matched them. That gap is published on the review page rather than papered over.
