Executive summary

The short version,
for someone with four minutes

What exists, what was verified, what could not be, and what the next decision is.

Written 20 November 2026 · one page, no forecast, no revenue claim

What was asked for

An Amazon affiliate publication covering five supplied links: product research, comparison content, buying guides, click measurement and a compliant disclosure layer, built as a live GitHub Pages site and merged without further input.

What was built

A 25-page static site with 5 buying guides, a five-row product register, a per-product record for each, a comparison table, 15 questions, 16 decisions, 20 suggestions, 17 items in the review queue, a source list with quoted wording, an empty campaign ledger, an inert analytics client and a build-time checker that fails on a broken link, an untagged Amazon link, a missing disclosure or a reintroduced price.

What was actually verified

All five supplied links were opened on 20 November 2026 and all five resolved to live Amazon listings, with each ASIN and full title transcribed. One of them had failed three times in an earlier session and resolved on both attempts in this one, which is recorded rather than smoothed over. The Associates Operating Agreement section 5, the Program Policies on Special Links and on price display, 16 CFR 255.0(f), Google's outbound-link documentation, and Amazon's own ASIN definition and application-review page were each read directly, and the relevant wording is quoted on the sources page. Four things changed as a result: the buy links are now tagged Special Links, the catalogue is three air fryers and two thermometers, a verification log is published, and a decision dossier sits above the suggestion queue.

The two findings that change the plan

The links were not Special Links. After the redirects were followed, every supplied link ended on a bare /dp/<ASIN> address with no tag, and the site was offering those addresses as click targets. Program Policies 2(b) requires the Associates ID as a parameter in the URL of each link, and Amazon's own review page lists untagged links among the reasons an application is rejected. The build now emits /dp/<ASIN>/?tag=shopnow047c-20 and fails if an untagged retail link appears. That is a defect fixed; attribution is still unproven until one click shows in the report.

Two of the five products are thermometers. P-04 resolved on retry to an instant-read probe. The catalogue is three air fryers and two thermometers, so every page that said otherwise — including this summary — was corrected, and the thermometers got a guide of their own.

What could not be verified, and why it matters

Tracking attribution
No arrival page showed a tag parameter. Whether clicks are credited to shopnow047c-20 is unknown. This is the difference between a site that earns and a site that does not.
Price, rating, availability
Not shown anywhere, because Amazon permits them only from Amazon-served data or its API. This is deliberate and is the site's largest visible gap to a reader.
Author
None. No name would be true, so none is used. It caps how much authority the guides can claim.
Product quality
Nothing was tested. No product is ranked, scored or recommended.
Traffic and revenue
No analytics are running, no Search Console property is verified, and no commission rate has been read. Therefore no conversion score and no revenue forecast appears anywhere on this site.

The next decision

Make one tagged click and read the Associates click report. That single action closes the highest-severity flag on the site, and it is the only thing standing between a finished publication and an earning one. Everything else is already as compliant as this repository can make it without account access.

After that, choose a path rather than a page. Path A proves the click and puts the shortlist above the method. Path B publishes to measured queries once Search Console is verified. Path C buys one appliance, uses it, and photographs it, which is the only route to a page a competitor cannot copy in an afternoon. Writing a sixth guide before one of those lands is the option that looks like progress and is not.

The full reasoning, with the missing information named row by row, is on Before you decide.
