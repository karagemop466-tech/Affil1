What changed in this pass

Seven decisions taken, with reasons

  1. Tagged links. Program Policies 2(b) requires the Associates ID as a URL parameter. The previous build linked to bare /dp/ addresses; every product link is now /dp/ASIN?tag=shopnow047c-20 (FLG-016).
  2. P-04 resolved and corrected. A third request reached B0DHZTCNR5, a ThermoMaven instant-read thermometer. The old "unresolved" text was removed from the product record and kept in the flag log (FLG-004).
  3. The front page leads with products. Three shapes of shop window, then the guides, then the machinery. The audit material moved to the review pages it belongs on.
  4. Guides are clustered. Shape, size, upkeep and thermometers, each with one anchor guide, so a new article has a home rather than joining a flat list.
  5. A thermometer guide exists, because two of five products are thermometers and the publication only spoke about air fryers.
  6. One origin, one index. The superseded parallel edition is marked noindex and excluded from the sitemap rather than deleted (FLG-018).
  7. The suggestion board is a decision tool. Fifteen proposals with cost, risk, evidence and dependencies, seven already implemented, each with an accept / hold / reject control.
