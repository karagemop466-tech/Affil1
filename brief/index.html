Executive summary

The short version,
for someone with four minutes

What exists, what was verified against an official source, what could not be, and what the next decision is. No forecasts, no revenue figures, no estimates.

Written 20 November 2026 · 5 products · 6 guides · 16 questions

What was asked for

An Amazon affiliate publication around five supplied product links: research, comparison content, buying guides, click measurement, a compliance layer and a live site on GitHub Pages, delivered without further input from the owner.

What exists now

A generated static site of 27 pages: a shop front, five product records, two category pages, %d question-shaped guides, a comparison table, a publishing calendar, %d open questions with the artefact that would close each one, a decision queue of proposals, a review queue of flags, a source register with quoted wording, an empty campaign ledger, and a measurement client plus collector that are written, tested and switched off.

Every page is built from one data file by a script, and a checker fails the build on a broken link, a missing disclosure, an untagged Amazon link or a reintroduced price.

What was verified, against what

Five supplied links
Each opened on 20 November 2026 and the destination and listing title transcribed. All five now resolve: four air-fryer and oven listings, plus two thermometers. Destination and ASIN for each are in the register.
The required disclosure sentence
Quoted verbatim from section 5 of the Associates Program Operating Agreement, updated 15 October 2025.
The link and price rules
Quoted from section 2 of the Associates Program Policies, updated 14 April 2026: the tag must appear as a URL parameter in each link, and price may only be shown when Amazon serves it or it arrives through the API.
Paid-link markup
rel="sponsored" as the marker for paid links, from Google Search Central.
Disclosure placement
FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ: a disclosure must be where it is hard to miss, which is why it sits above the masthead on every page and not only in the footer.
Food-safety temperatures
The only numbers on the site quoted as fact rather than as a seller's claim, from the USDA FSIS chart, last updated 14 April 2025.

The finding that changed the build

Before this pass, 48 of the site's 101 Amazon product links carried no tracking tag. They were correctly marked sponsored and nofollow, but a click through them could not be attributed to shopnow047c-20, and Amazon's Commission Income Statement excludes purchases that are not correctly tracked because a link was not properly formatted.

The cause was one line: the template passed the untagged listing URL instead of the tagged one. Every product link now passes through a single function that appends the tag, and a build check fails if any Amazon product link on any generated page lacks one. What is still unconfirmed is whether a real click is credited inside the account, which only the Associates report can show.

What could not be verified, and why it matters

Tracking attribution
The tag is now applied to every link, but no click has been observed in the Associates report. This is the difference between a site that can earn and one that only looks like it can.
Intended ASIN per link
The supplied codes are nine-character slugs. The product each link was meant to reach can only be confirmed from the owner's account, so it is recorded as unknown rather than guessed.
Price, rating, review count, stock
Absent site-wide by rule, because neither permitted data path is connected.
Product quality
Nothing was bought, used or measured. No product is ranked, scored or named a winner.
Author
No byline. Naming someone would be misleading, and it is the site's clearest credibility ceiling.
Traffic, conversion, revenue
No analytics are running, no Search Console property is verified, no commission rate has been read. Therefore no conversion score, no revenue forecast and no campaign row exists.

The next three decisions, in order

  1. Confirm one click in the Associates report. Tagged links exist; evidence they are credited does not. One click, then the report for that day.
  2. Put the site into two indexes. Verify Search Console and submit the sitemap, then submit the same sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools. Both accept a sitemap and report queries without any script on the page.
  3. Then choose between testing and waiting. Either buy one product and build a page around real measurements, or wait four weeks for query data and write the next guide from that. Writing another guide from instinct is the option that looks like progress and is not.
