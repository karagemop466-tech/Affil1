Executive summary

The short version,
for someone with four minutes

What exists, what was verified, what could not be, and what the next decision is.

Written 20 November 2026 · checked against the sources listed below

What was asked for

An Amazon affiliate publication covering five supplied links: product research, comparison content, buying guides, click measurement and a compliant disclosure layer, built as a live GitHub Pages site and merged without further input.

What was built

A single canonical static site: a product-led front page, five picks in two categories, 10 question-shaped guides, a per-product note for every item, a comparison table, a decision register of 24 questions, a suggestion queue with three paths, 27 entries in the review queue, a source list with quoted wording, an empty campaign ledger, an inert analytics client and a build-time checker that fails on a broken link, a missing disclosure, an unapproved tagged Amazon link or a reintroduced price.

What was actually verified

Each of the five supplied links was opened on 20 November 2026 and all five resolved to an Amazon listing. The listing title for each is in the table at the foot of this page. Amazon's Associates Operating Agreement section 5, its Program Policies sections 2(a) and 2(b), the FTC's endorsement FAQ and Google's outbound-link documentation were read on 20 November 2026. Quoted wording appears on the sources page only where it matched the text read; the FTC quotes did not match and are blank there.

The one finding that changes the plan

The links on this site previously carried no tracking parameter at all, which meant clicks could not be attributed to any account. The five supplied link codes are nine characters and contain lowercase letters, while every ASIN that loaded is ten and uppercase: they are short-link slugs, not identifiers, so the intended product for each link cannot be read from the link and has to come from the Associates dashboard.

Product links are listing citations until an exact Special Link has dated intent and reporting evidence. Credited clicks are not confirmed.

What could not be verified, and why it matters

Tracking attribution
No arrival page showed a tag parameter. Whether clicks are credited to shopnow047c-20 is unknown. This is the difference between a site that earns and a site that does not.
Price, rating, availability
Not shown anywhere, because Amazon permits them only from Amazon-served data or its API. This is deliberate and is the site's largest visible gap to a reader.
Author
None. No name would be true, so none is used. It caps how much authority the guides can claim.
Product quality
Nothing was tested. No product is ranked, scored or recommended.
Traffic and revenue
No analytics are running, no Search Console property is verified, and no commission rate has been read. Therefore no conversion score and no revenue forecast appears anywhere on this site.

The next decision

Three things, in this order. Make one test click from the live site, then read the Associates report for that day. Verify the Search Console property and submit the sitemap. Then choose a path on the suggestions page: shop-first, publication-first or measure-first — and commit to one before adding anything else.

Writing more guides first is the option that looks like progress and is not.
