Editorial brief for the next guide

Pick a meal the reader actually cooks. Open with one concrete decision, such as whether two foods finish together or whether the tray clears the door. Give a measuring step, a trade-off and a reason not to buy. Link to the relevant comparison or listing. Do not use “best,” trend claims, prices, ratings or a hands-on voice without evidence.

Candidate questions: Will two different sides fit without a second batch? and Do I need a leave-in probe or just a quick spot-check? These are ideas, not verified demand. Review them against measured queries before scheduling new pieces.
