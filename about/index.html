Crisp & Counter is a static publication about air fryers and meat thermometers. It has no backend, no cookies and no third-party requests. This page states its rules so you can hold it to them.

Static HTML · self-hosted fonts · no analytics running · no prices

What this site is

A buyer-facing guide to five verified Amazon listings across two complementary categories: 3 Air Fryers and 2 Meat Thermometers. Every submitted link was opened against Amazon.com, its observed ASIN and category breadcrumb recorded, and its seller-stated attributes separated cleanly from unverified fields.

It is not a hands-on testing lab. Nothing here was bought, cooked with or measured. Safe minimum internal cooking temperatures are cited directly from USDA FSIS (SRC-06, SRC-07), and that limit is stated plainly on every product page and guide.

Why so much is blank

Amazon’s Associates Program Policies, section 2(b), permit showing prices and availability only if Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or if the site obtains it through the Creators API or the Product Advertising API (SRC-02). This build has access to neither. So price, star rating, review count and stock status are absent everywhere — by rule, not by omission.

The same discipline applies to fields nobody regulates: dimensions, wattage, probe calibration and warranty terms were not confirmed from an official specification sheet in this build, so they stay blank until verified.

How the links work

Every link to an Amazon product listing on this site carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and opens in a new tab. Google’s own documentation confirms that sponsored is the documented marker for paid or advertising links (SRC-05). That rule is enforced automatically by tools/check.py.

The tracking ID supplied for this site is shopnow047c-20. Whether clicks on the supplied short links are credited to it has not been confirmed, because confirming attribution requires a test click and a check inside Associates Central (FLG-002).

The disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That exact sentence is required by section 5 of the Associates Program Operating Agreement (SRC-01). It appears in a disclosure strip directly beneath the masthead above the fold on every page, repeats in every footer, and sits beside outbound product links per FTC Endorsement Guides guidance (SRC-03, SRC-04).

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, calls no external APIs and embeds no fonts from a CDN. The typefaces are self-hosted in this repository. The analytics client is present and inert by default; no data leaves the browser. There is no form, no newsletter and no comment system.

Imagery

All editorial photographs are AI-generated illustrations, visibly labelled where they appear and never presented as photographs of the actual products. Every asset is listed with the rule it follows.

Who wrote this

No author is named, because inventing a persona would be deceptive. This authority limit is recorded as FLG-006, and naming a real editor with an accurate bio is proposal S-03.
