Crisp & Counter is a static publication about air fryers and one thermometer. It has no backend, no cookies and no third-party requests. This page states its rules so you can hold it to them.

Static HTML · self-hosted fonts · no analytics running · no prices

What this site is

A short guide to five kitchen things, written for someone standing in a kitchen with a tape measure and a decision to make. Three air fryers and two meat thermometers were submitted by the operator of the site. Each link was opened, followed to its Amazon listing, and the destination recorded. The guides around them describe what to check, in what order, and why.

It is not a review site. Nothing here was bought, cooked with, measured or compared by testing. That is a real limitation and it is stated on every product page and in every guide rather than in a footnote here.

Why so much is blank

Amazon's Associates Program Policies, section 2(b), say that a site may show prices and availability only if Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or if the site obtains it through the Creators API or the Product Advertising API. This build has access to neither. So price, star rating, review count and stock status are absent everywhere — not because they are difficult to copy, but because copying them is the specific thing that clause forbids.

The same discipline applies to fields nobody regulates: dimensions, wattage and warranty terms were not confirmed from an official specification sheet in this build, so they are empty too. An empty cell is a question mark. A filled-in cell that nobody verified is a lie with a font.

One site, not two

Until 20 November 2026 this repository served two competing editions of the same shop: a generated one at the root and a hand-written one in a subdirectory, with duplicate pages covering the same five products. Two editions cannot both be the answer a search engine should index. The root build is now canonical, the other edition is preserved under _archive/legacy-2026-11-20/ with a noindex tag on every page, and the sitemap lists only the canonical URLs. Nothing was deleted; the archive and the git history both keep it.

How the links work

Only approved Special Links may carry the selected Associates tag. Listing citations are untagged. Every outbound product link is marked rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and opens in a new tab. Google's own documentation on qualifying outbound links confirms that sponsored is the correct marker for paid or advertising links. That is applied by the build script to every Amazon anchor at once, and a check fails if any is missing it.

The tracking ID supplied for this site is shopnow047c-20. Whether a click is actually credited to it has not been confirmed, because confirming it needs a click from the operator's own device followed by a look at the Associates report. That is on the review queue as FLG-002 rather than being asserted here.

The disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That exact sentence is required by section 5 of the Associates Program Operating Agreement, and it appears in a strip above the masthead on every page of this site, and again in the footer. The FTC's guidance says a disclosure at the bottom of a page is easy to miss and that the closer a disclosure sits to the recommendation, the better — so every product card also carries its own one-line affiliate note, and every product page repeats it beside the button.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, calls no external APIs and embeds no fonts from a CDN. The typefaces are served from this repository. The analytics client is present but inert: it writes to session storage and transmits nothing. There is no form, no newsletter and no comment system, so the site holds no personal data at all.

Imagery

All eight images are AI-generated illustrations, labelled where they appear and never presented as a photograph of a product. Every asset is listed with the rule it follows.

Who wrote this

No author is named, because there is no person whose name would be accurate to put here. This is the site's largest weakness and it is recorded as FLG-006 rather than papered over with a stock biography and an invented credential. A named editor with a true description of what they did is suggestion S-03.
