How this shop works

A shop is a set of promises about what it will and will not do. These are ours, in plain language, with the reasoning attached.

The disclosure, and why it is at the top of every page

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That sentence appears directly under the masthead on every page of this site, and again in the footer. It is the exact wording Amazon's Associates Operating Agreement requires, and the placement is deliberate: the FTC's guidance for endorsements says a disclosure is likely to be missed if it appears only on an about page, at the end of a post, or anywhere that requires a click.

So it is not on this page alone. It is on all of them, above the fold, before any product.

Every outbound product link is marked as sponsored

Each button that leaves this site for Amazon carries the Associates ID shopnow047c-20 in its url and is marked rel="sponsored" in the page markup. Each one names its destination on the button itself, rather than hiding behind a shortened link.

What we publish, and what we deliberately do not

Product detailsQuoted from the listing title and feature text, with the ASIN shown so you can check it.
PricesNot published. Amazon permits showing a price only when Amazon serves it or when it comes from an approved API. Prices also change hourly, so a copied price is a wrong price.
Star ratings and review quotesNot published. Amazon restricts the display of customer reviews and star ratings to data obtained through its APIs. The live listing shows them; this site links there.
Hands-on testingNot claimed. No product here has been tested by this desk. The FTC is explicit that you cannot describe experience with a product you have not tried, so the copy is written as comparison criteria instead of verdicts.
Rankings and "best of" listsNot published. A ranking implies a comparison that was actually performed. These pages help you eliminate options; they do not crown a winner.

How products get on the shelf

A link is submitted, the destination is checked on a dated pass, and a page is built only for links that resolve to a listing the shop can describe. Where a link does not resolve, it is held and published as a defect rather than guessed at.

The shop currently stocks four products from five submitted links. What happened to the fifth is documented on the held link page.

What we cannot yet tell you

Several things a reader might reasonably want to know are not established, and rather than paper over them they are listed in public:

  • Which exact product each submitted link was intended to promote. The destinations were observed; intent has not been confirmed.
  • Whether the short-link format used in the submission is itself a compliant link format. That question is open, not answered.
  • Who writes this desk. The byline is an organisation name because no named author was supplied.

All of it sits on the open questions page and the Flagged for Review page.

Measurement, and what we do not collect

This site counts outbound clicks without a cookie, without a session identifier, and without collecting an IP address or user agent. It runs no third-party analytics script. The counter is described in full on the measurement page — including the fact that it is currently switched off because no endpoint has been configured.

The records

Everything behind the shop is published: the source register with a link for every verified claim, the product intent record, the compliance checks, the campaign ledger (deliberately empty), the suggestion register, and the flagged items.
