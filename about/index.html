Crisp & Counter is a static publication about three air fryers and two thermometers. No backend, no cookies, no third-party requests. This page states the rules so you can hold the site to them.

Static HTML · self-hosted fonts · no analytics running · no prices or ratings

What this site is

A short guide to a small number of kitchen tools, written for someone standing in a kitchen with a tape measure and a decision to make. 5 links were supplied by the operator. Each was opened and its destination recorded. The guides around them describe what to check, in what order, and why.

It is not a review site. Nothing here was bought, cooked with, measured or compared by testing. That limitation is stated on every product page and inside every guide, not only here.

Why so much is blank

Amazon's Associates Program Policies, section 2(b), say a site may show prices and availability only where Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or where the site obtains it through the Creators API or the Product Advertising API. This build has access to neither, so price, star rating, review count and stock status are absent everywhere — not because they are hard to copy, but because copying them is the specific thing that clause forbids.

The same discipline applies to fields nobody regulates: dimensions, wattage and warranty terms were not confirmed from an official specification sheet here, so they are empty too. An empty cell is a question mark; a filled-in cell nobody verified is a lie with a font.

How the links work

Every Amazon product link on this site is a tagged Special Link: the observed ASIN plus ?tag=shopnow047c-20, which is the parameter form Program Policies 2(b) describes. Each one carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener", opens in a new tab, and says so in its accessible label. Google documents sponsored as the marker for paid links.

Whether a click is actually credited to the ID has not been confirmed, because confirming it needs one click from the operator's device followed by a look at the Associates report. That is FLG-002 and Q-08 on the review queue rather than an assertion here.

The disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That exact sentence is required by section 5 of the Operating Agreement. It appears in a strip under the masthead on every page and again in the footer. The FTC's guidance says a disclosure at the bottom of a page is easy to miss and that it should sit close to the recommendation — so every product card also carries its own line, and every product page repeats it beside the button.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, calls no external APIs and embeds no fonts from a CDN. The typefaces are served from this repository. The analytics client is present but inert: it transmits nothing unless an endpoint is configured, and none is. There is no form and no newsletter, so the site holds no personal data at all.

If a reader marks decisions on the suggestions page, that choice is stored in their own browser and never sent anywhere.

Imagery

Every photograph here is an AI-generated illustration, labelled where it appears and never presented as a photograph of a product. None carries a brand mark. All eleven assets are listed with the rule each one follows and the reason for it.

The publishing gate

No page publishes unless the build passes. tools/check.py fails on a missing or misplaced disclosure, an Amazon link without sponsored, any price or rating phrase in visible text, any root-absolute internal link, any broken link or anchor, any external resource load, more than one h1, and any image without alt text. The same script runs in GitHub Actions before deployment, and a reproducibility step fails the build if a generated page was edited by hand.

Who wrote this

No author is named, because there is no person whose name would be accurate here. This is the site's largest weakness and it is recorded as FLG-019 rather than papered over with a stock biography. Supplying a name and two true sentences would fix it (S-12).
